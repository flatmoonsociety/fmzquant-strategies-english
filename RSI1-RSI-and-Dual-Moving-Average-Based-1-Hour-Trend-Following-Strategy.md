#### Overview
This strategy uses the relative strength index (RSI) and two simple moving averages (SMA) as the main indicators to generate long and short signals on the 1-hour time frame. By relaxing the condition settings of RSI and SMA, the frequency of signal triggering is increased. At the same time, the strategy also uses the average true range (ATR) indicator for risk management and dynamically sets take-profit and stop-loss levels.
The main ideas of this strategy are as follows:
1. Use the RSI indicator to identify potential overbought and oversold conditions as signals to go long and short respectively.
2. Use the intersection of fast SMA and slow SMA to determine the potential upward trend (golden cross) and downward trend (death cross).
3. When RSI and SMA meet the long or short conditions at the same time, open a position in the corresponding direction.
4. Use the ATR indicator to calculate dynamic take-profit and stop-loss levels to control the risk of each transaction.
5. The triggering status of the strategy signal is visually displayed through changes in the background color of the chart, making it easy to debug and understand the strategy logic.
#### Strategy Principle
1. RSI indicator: When RSI is below 50, it indicates that the market may be oversold and the price has the potential to rise, thus triggering a long signal; when RSI is above 50, it indicates that the market may be overbought and the price has the potential to fall, thus triggering a short signal.
2. Double moving average crossover: When the fast SMA crosses above the slow SMA (golden cross), it indicates a potential upward trend and triggers a long signal; when the fast SMA crosses below the slow SMA (a dead cross), it indicates a potential downward trend and triggers a short signal.
3. Position opening conditions: Only when RSI and the double moving average meet the long or short conditions at the same time, the position in the corresponding direction will be opened to improve the reliability of the signal.
4. Risk management: Use the ATR indicator to calculate the dynamic take-profit and stop-loss levels. The take-profit level is set to the opening price plus/minus 1.5 times ATR, and the stop-loss level is set to the opening price plus/minus 1 time ATR. In this way, stop-profit and stop-loss can be dynamically adjusted according to market fluctuations and the risk of each transaction can be controlled.
#### Strategic Advantages
1. Strong adaptability: By relaxing the condition settings of RSI and double moving averages, the strategy can adapt to different market conditions within the 1-hour time frame and capture more trading opportunities.
2. Risk management: Use the ATR indicator to dynamically set take-profit and stop-loss levels, which can be flexibly adjusted according to market fluctuations and effectively control the risk exposure of each transaction.
3. Simple and easy to use: The strategy logic is clear and the indicators used are simple and easy to understand and implement.
4. Visual aid: The strategy signal is visually displayed through the change of chart background color, which facilitates debugging and optimization.
#### Strategy Risk
1. Frequent trading: Due to the relaxed condition setting of RSI and double moving average, the strategy may produce more frequent trading signals, which will increase transaction costs and affect overall returns.
2. Consolidating market: In a consolidating market with less volatility, RSI and double moving averages may produce frequent false signals, resulting in poor strategy performance.
3. Lack of trend: The strategy mainly relies on RSI and double moving averages to judge the trend, but in some cases, the market may not have obvious trend characteristics, causing the strategy signal to be invalid.
4. Parameter sensitivity: The performance of the strategy may be more sensitive to the parameter settings of indicators such as RSI, SMA and ATR. Different parameter combinations may lead to large differences in strategy performance.
#### Strategy optimization direction
1. Parameter optimization: Optimize the parameters of indicators such as RSI, SMA and ATR to find the parameter combination that performs best on historical data to improve the stability and reliability of the strategy.
2. Signal filtering: Introduce other technical indicators or market sentiment indicators to conduct secondary confirmation of signals generated by RSI and double moving averages to reduce the occurrence of false signals.
3. Dynamic weight adjustment: Dynamically adjust the weight of RSI and double moving average signals according to the strength of the market trend. Give a higher weight when the trend is obvious, reduce the weight in a consolidating market, and improve the adaptability of the strategy.
4. Take-profit and stop-loss optimization: Optimize the ATR multiple, find the best take-profit and stop-loss ratio, and improve the risk-adjusted return of the strategy. At the same time, you can consider introducing other take-profit and stop-loss methods, such as take-profit and stop-loss based on support/resistance levels, or time-based take-profit and stop-loss.
5. Multi-time frame analysis: Combined with signals from other time frames (such as 4-hour, daily, etc.), filter and confirm signals from the 1-hour time frame to improve the reliability of the signal.

This strategy combines two simple and easy-to-use technical indicators, RSI and dual moving averages, to generate trend following signals on the 1-hour time frame, while utilizing the ATR indicator for dynamic risk management. > Source (PineScript)

``` pinescript
/*backtest
start: 2024-02-01 00:00:00
end: 2024-02-29 23:59:59
period: 1h
basePeriod: 15m
exchanges: [{"eid":"Futures_Binance","currency":"BTC_USDT"}]
*/

//@version=5
strategy("Debugged 1H Strategy with Liberal Conditions", shorttitle="1H Debug", overlay=true, pyramiding=0)

// Parameters
rsiLength = input.int(14, title="RSI Length")
rsiLevel = input.int(50, title="RSI Entry Level") // More likely to be met than the previous 70
fastLength = input.int(10, title="Fast MA Length")
slowLength = input.int(21, title="Slow MA Length")
atrLength = input.int(14, title="ATR Length")
atrMultiplier = input.float(1.5, title="ATR Multiplier for SL")
riskRewardMultiplier = input.float(2, title="Risk/Reward Multiplier")

// Indicators
rsi = ta.rsi(close, rsiLength)
fastMA = ta.sma(close, fastLength)
slowMA = ta.sma(close, slowLength)
atr = ta.atr(atrLength)

// Trades
longCondition = ta.crossover(fastMA, slowMA) and rsi < rsiLevel
shortCondition = ta.crossunder(fastMA, slowMA) and rsi > rsiLevel

// Entry and Exit Logic
if (longCondition)
    strategy.entry("Long", strategy.long)
    strategy.exit("Exit Long", "Long", profit=atrMultiplier * atr, loss=atr)

if (shortCondition)
    strategy.entry("Short", strategy.short)
    strategy.exit("Exit Short", "Short", profit=atrMultiplier * atr, loss=atr)

// Debugging: Visualize when conditions are met
bgcolor(longCondition ? color.new(color.green, 90) : na)
bgcolor(shortCondition ? color.new(color.red, 90) : na)
```

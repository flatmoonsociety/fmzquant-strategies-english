####Overview
This strategy is based on the AHR999 indicator and AHR999X indicator. By comparing the relationship between the current price and the harmonized average price and logarithmic price, it determines whether Bitcoin is in an overbought or oversold state, and conducts fixed investment and bargain hunting operations accordingly. At the same time, the strategy also sets a profit-taking condition and performs partial sales when the AHR999X indicator falls below a certain threshold.
####Strategy Principle
1. Calculate the harmonic average of the historical price of Bitcoin as a reference for the long-term equilibrium price.
2. Calculate the corresponding logarithmic price based on the number of days since Bitcoin was born, as a reference for long-term trend prices.
3. Calculate the current AHR999 indicator, which is the product of the ratio of the current price to the harmonic average price and the logarithmic price.
4. Calculate the current AHR999X indicator, that is, the ratio of the harmonic average price to the current price multiplied by 3 times the ratio of the logarithmic price to the current price.
5. Based on the value of the AHR999 indicator, determine whether to make fixed investment or bargain hunting operations. When AHR999 is within the set range, make a fixed investment, and when it is below the lower limit, make a bargain.
6. Based on the value of the AHR999X indicator, determine whether to perform a stop-profit operation. When AHR999X is lower than the set threshold, a certain proportion of the position will be sold.
####Advantage analysis
1. The strategy is based on the long-term equilibrium price and trend price of Bitcoin, and has a certain theoretical basis and interpretability.
2. The harmonic mean is introduced to reduce the impact of short-term fluctuations and make the judgment more robust.
3. Through the combination of AHR999 and AHR999X, the overbought and oversold status of Bitcoin and the timing of taking profits can be better captured.
4. Combining fixed investment and bargain hunting, gradually building a position when the price is relatively underestimated, the cost is relatively low.
5. By setting up a profit stop operation, you can obtain a certain amount of income when the price is relatively overvalued, while retaining most of your positions to obtain long-term appreciation.
####Risk Analysis
1. The strategy relies on the AHR999 and AHR999X indicators. If the trend of Bitcoin price changes significantly in the future, causing these two indicators to become invalid, the strategy may face greater risks.
2. The harmonic average is more sensitive to the length of historical prices. If the selected historical price range is not reasonable enough, it may affect the strategy performance.
3. The calculation of logarithmic price is based on the number of days since the birth of Bitcoin. If the long-term growth trend of Bitcoin changes, the logarithmic price may lose its reference significance.
4. When the price is at a relatively high level, the strategy may face the risk of insufficient funds for bargain hunting, resulting in the inability to open a position in time.
5. The take-profit operation may cause the strategy to miss part of the profits if the price continues to rise.
####Optimization direction
1. Optimize the thresholds of AHR999 and AHR999X indicators to find the most suitable fixed investment, bottom buying and profit taking ranges.
2. Introduce more indicators, such as trading volume, volatility, etc., to assist in judging trends and overbought and oversold conditions.
3. Optimize the calculation method of the harmonic average, such as appropriately adjusting the weight of historical prices and reducing reliance on long-term historical prices.
4. Dynamically adjust the amount of fixed investment and bargain hunting, increase investment when the price is low, and reduce investment when the price is high.
5. Optimize the profit-taking operation, such as dynamically adjusting according to price trends and position costs to balance returns and risks.
####Summary
The AHR999 mean reversion strategy determines the overbought and oversold status of Bitcoin by comparing the current price with the historical equilibrium price and trend price, and performs fixed investment, bottom hunting and profit taking operations. This strategy has a certain theoretical basis and interpretability. Through the combination of the two indicators, it can better capture the price fluctuation patterns of Bitcoin. However, the strategy also faces risks in indicator failure, parameter selection, fund management, etc., and needs to be continuously optimized and improved in practice. In general, the AHR999 mean reversion strategy provides a feasible idea for long-term value investment in Bitcoin.

function harmonicMean(x) {
	if (x.length === 0) {
		return undefined;
	}
	var reciprocalSum = 0;
	for (var i = 0; i < x.length; i++) {
		if (x[i] <= 0) {
			return undefined;
		}
		reciprocalSum += 1 / x[i];
	}
	return x.length / reciprocalSum;
}

function CalcAHR999() {
	var now = parseInt(Unix());
	var prices = GetPrices();
	var avgPrice = harmonicMean(prices);
	var nowPrice = GetNowPrice();
	var birthday = (now - 1230940800) / (24 * 60 * 60);

		logPrice = Math.pow(10, 5.8 * Math.log10(parseInt(birthday)) - 16.88);

	var ahr999x = Math.round((avgPrice / nowPrice) * (logPrice / nowPrice) * 3 * 1000) / 1000;
	return {
		ahr999: ahr999,
		ahr999x: ahr999x,
	};
}

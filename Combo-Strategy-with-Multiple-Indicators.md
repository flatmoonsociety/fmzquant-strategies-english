## Overview
This strategy uses a combination of different types of technical indicators to generate more accurate and reliable trading signals. The strategy consists of two parts: the first part uses the 123 reversal strategy and the second part uses the TEMA indicator. The trading signals generated by the two are combined to filter out erroneous signals and improve signal quality.
## Strategy Principle
The first part uses the 123 reversal strategy. This strategy is based on the stochastic indicator. When the stock price reverses the closing price for two consecutive days (for example, the closing price of the two days changes from rising to falling), and the stochastic indicator shows an overbought or oversold signal, a buy or sell signal is generated.
Specifically, if the closing price is higher than the previous day's closing price and the stochastic slow line is below 50, a buy signal is generated. If the closing price is lower than the previous day's closing price and the stochastic line is above 50, a sell signal is generated.
The second part uses the TEMA indicator. TEMA stands for Triple Exponential Moving Average and is calculated as:
TEMA = 3*EMA(CLOSE, N) - 3*EMA(EMA(CLOSE, N), N) + EMA(EMA(EMA(CLOSE, N), N), N)

Among them, N is the parameter length. If the closing price is higher than the TEMA indicator value, a buy signal is generated; if the closing price is lower than the TEMA indicator value, a sell signal is generated.
Finally, combine the signals of both indicators. Only when two indicators produce consistent signals (both buy or both sell), an actual trading signal is generated.
## Strategic Advantages
- By combining multiple indicators, some false signals can be filtered out and signal quality improved.
- 123 The reversal strategy is based on the actual reversal of the stock price to avoid chasing high buying and panic selling in the trend.
- TEMA can reduce noise through multiple smoothing and produce more reliable signals.
- The combination of two different types of indicators can ensure the reliability of trading signals to a greater extent.
## Risk and Optimization
- Reversal strategies inherently carry the risk of missing the larger upward trend in a bull market. The parameters of the reversal strategy can be appropriately adjusted to reduce the probability of missing out.
- TEMA, as a trend following indicator, has the possibility of generating false signals. The parameters of TEMA can be appropriately adjusted to optimize the sensitivity of the indicator. 
- If the parameters of the two indicators are selected improperly, the signals may be too sparse. Parameters need to be adjusted to achieve appropriate trading frequency.
- You can consider adding filters to turn off the strategy in specific market environments to control risks.
## Summarize
This strategy improves the quality of trading signals through a reasonable combination of multiple indicators, and is a very effective quantitative trading strategy. However, it is still necessary to pay attention to risk control, appropriately adjust parameters or add other components for optimization, so that the strategy can operate stably in different markets.

// Second strategy
// This study plots the TEMA1 indicator. TEMA1 ia s triple MA (Moving Average),

Reversal123(Length, KSmoothing, DLength, Level) =>
    vFast = sma(stoch(close, high, low, Length), KSmoothing) 
    vSlow = sma(vFast, DLength)
    pos = 0.0
    pos := iff(close[2] < close[1] and close > close[1] and vFast < vSlow and vFast > Level, 1,
	         iff(close[2] > close[1] and close < close[1] and vFast > vSlow and vFast < Level, -1, nz(pos[1], 0))) 
	pos


TEMA(Length) =>
    pos = 0.0
    xPrice = close
    xEMA1 = ema(xPrice, Length)
    xEMA2 = ema(xEMA1, Length)
    xEMA3 = ema(xEMA2, Length)
    nRes = 3 * xEMA1 - 3 * xEMA2 + xEMA3
    pos := iff(close > nRes, 1,
             iff(close < nRes, -1, nz(pos[1], 0))) 
    pos

	   iff(posReversal123 == -1 and posT3A == -1, -1, 0))

          iff(reverse and pos == -1 , 1, pos))	   
if (possig == 1 ) 
    strategy.entry("Long", strategy.long)
if (possig == -1 )
    strategy.entry("Short", strategy.short)	 
if (possig == 0) 
    strategy.close_all()
barcolor(possig == -1 ? #b50404: possig == 1 ? #079605 : #0536b3 )

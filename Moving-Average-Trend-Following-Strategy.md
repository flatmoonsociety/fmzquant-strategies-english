## Overview
This strategy calculates and compares the fast moving average (Fast MA) and the slow moving average (Slow MA) to determine the direction of the market trend and implement long or short positions by following the trend. When the fast moving average crosses the slow moving average, go long; when the fast moving average crosses below the slow moving average, go short. Set stop loss and take profit at the same time to control risks.
## Principle
The core logic of this strategy is based on the golden cross of the moving average. Moving averages can well reflect the changing trend of the average market price. The fast average line is shorter and can respond quickly to price changes; the slow average line is longer and represents a greater degree of the market's trend direction. When the fast moving average crosses the slow moving average, it means that the market has entered a bullish trend; when the fast moving average crosses below the slow moving average, it means that the market has entered a bearish trend.
Specifically, this strategy calculates fast and slow moving averages with lengths of 50 and 200 periods respectively. At the close of each K line, determine whether the fast moving average crosses above or below the slow moving average. If an upward crossing occurs (the yellow line crosses the red line), a long order will be entered at the market price when the next K line opens; if a downward crossing occurs (the yellow line crosses below the red line), a short order will be entered at the market price when the next K line opens.
After entering the position, TrailStop will be used to track the stop loss and lock in the profit. In addition, the value based on ATR is also set to determine the stop loss and take profit levels.
## Advantages
This is a more typical trend following strategy with the following advantages:
1. Using moving averages to determine the trend direction has higher accuracy and better winning rate.
2. Using a combination of moving averages with different speeds can effectively filter market noise and capture the main trends.
3. Set stop-loss and stop-profit levels to control single losses and increase the probability of profit.
4. The backtesting effect is good, the maximum retracement and Sharpe rate acceptance levels
5. The strategy logic is simple and easy to understand, and the parameters can be adjusted flexibly, making it suitable for ordinary traders.
## Risk
This strategy also has the following risks:
1. When the market fluctuates violently, the signals generated by the moving average may lag behind and be susceptible to false breakthroughs.
2. Improper stop loss or take profit setting may lead to loss or failure
3. Too much reliance on parameter settings. Improper parameters will greatly affect the strategy effect.
4. Small losses caused by price probes and callbacks cannot be perfectly avoided.
5. Failure to consider fundamentals and the impact of major news events on the market
Corresponding solutions:

2. Use adaptive stop loss and take profit methods to avoid manual setting errors
3. Optimize parameters through complexity analysis and backtesting

//@version=4
strategy("EURCHF Smart Money Strategy", overlay=true)

// Input Parameters
fastLength = input(50, title="Fast MA Length")
slowLength = input(200, title="Slow MA Length")
riskRewardRatio = input(2, title="Risk-Reward Ratio")

// Calculate Moving Averages
fastMA = sma(close, fastLength)
slowMA = sma(close, slowLength)

// Strategy Conditions
longCondition = crossover(fastMA, slowMA)
shortCondition = crossunder(fastMA, slowMA)

// Execute Strategy
strategy.entry("Long", strategy.long, when = longCondition)
strategy.entry("Short", strategy.short, when = shortCondition)

// Set Stop Loss and Take Profit
atrValue = atr(14)
stopLoss = atrValue * 1
takeProfit = atrValue * riskRewardRatio

strategy.exit("ExitLong", from_entry="Long", loss=stopLoss, profit=takeProfit)
strategy.exit("ExitShort", from_entry="Short", loss=stopLoss, profit=takeProfit)

// Plot Moving Averages
plot(fastMA, color=color.green, title="Fast MA")
plot(slowMA, color=color.red, title="Slow MA")

This strategy comprehensively uses double moving averages and RSI indicators to construct a long-short crossover trading strategy. This strategy can capture medium and long-term trends while using short-term indicators to avoid unnecessary shocks.
#### Strategy Principle
This strategy uses two sets of moving averages, namely the fast moving average (EMA 59 and EMA 82) and the slow moving average (EMA 96 and EMA 95). When the price crosses the fast moving average from bottom to top, go long; when the price crosses the fast moving average from top to bottom, go short. At the same time, the overbought and oversold areas of the RSI indicator are used to confirm trading signals and stop losses.
Specifically, a long signal is generated when the fast EMA breaks above the slow EMA. At this time, if the RSI is below 30 (oversold area), enter the market long. When the fast EMA breaks below the slow EMA, a short signal is generated. If the RSI is above 70 (overbought area) at this time, enter the market short.
The advantage of using double moving averages is that it can better identify changes in medium and long-term trends. The RSI indicator can filter out some noise trading caused by false breakthroughs.
#### Strategic Advantages
- Use double moving averages to capture medium and long-term trends
- RSI indicator filters out noise trades
- Combine trend following and reversal trading
- Transaction logic is simple and clear
#### Risk Analysis
- In a volatile market, trading signals generated by moving averages may be deceived.
- The RSI indicator also fails under certain market conditions
- Stop loss points need to be set carefully to avoid being too loose or too tight.
#### Strategy optimization direction
- Test moving average combinations with longer periods
- Try different parameter adjustments, such as changes in RSI bullish and bearish ranges
- Add additional filtering conditions, such as trading volume indicators, etc.
- Optimize the stop loss strategy and combine dynamic stop loss with ATR and other indicators
#### Summarize
This strategy integrates dual moving average trend following with reversal trading of the RSI indicator. The double EMA tracks the medium and long-term trend direction, and the RSI is used to confirm the validity of trading signals and stop losses. This is a simple and practical long-short crossover strategy that can be adapted to different market environments through parameter adjustment and optimization.

//@version=2
strategy("Swing Hull/rsi/EMA Strategy", overlay=true,default_qty_type=strategy.cash,default_qty_value=10000,scale=true,initial_capital=10000,currency=currency.USD)

//A Swing trading strategy that use a combination of indicators, rsi for target, hull for overall direction enad ema for entering the martket.
// hull ma copied from syrowof HullMA who copied from mohamed982 :) thanks both
// Performance 

n=input(title="period",defval=500)

n2ma=2*wma(close,round(n/2))
nma=wma(close,n)
diff=n2ma-nma
sqn=round(sqrt(n))

n2ma1=2*wma(close[1],round(n/2))
nma1=wma(close[1],n)
diff1=n2ma1-nma1
sqn1=round(sqrt(n))

n1=wma(diff,sqn)
n2=wma(diff1,sqn)
c=n1>n2?green:red
ma=plot(n1,color=c)



// RSi and Moving averages

length = input( 14 )
overSold = input( 70)
overBought = input( 30)
point = 0.0001
dev= 2

fastLength = input(59)
fastLengthL = input(82)
slowLength = input(96)
slowLengthL = input(95)
price = close

mafast = ema(price, fastLength)
mafastL= ema(price, fastLengthL)
maslow = ema(price, slowLength)
maslowL = ema(price, slowLengthL)
vrsi = rsi(price, length)
cShort =  (crossunder(vrsi, overBought))

condDown = n2 >= n1
condUp = condDown != true
closeLong =  (crossover(vrsi, overSold))
closeShort = cShort 


// Strategy Logic
longCondition = n1> n2
shortCondition = longCondition != true

col =condUp ? lime : condDown ? red : yellow
plot(n1,color=col,linewidth=3)


if (not na(vrsi))
    if shortCondition    
        if (price[0] < maslow[0] and price[1] > mafast[1]) //cross entry
            strategy.entry("SYS-SHORT", strategy.short, comment="short")
strategy.close("SYS-SHORT", when=closeShort) //output logic

if (not na(vrsi))
    if longCondition // swing condition          
        if (price[0] < mafast[0] and price[1] > mafast[1]) //cross entry
            strategy.entry("SYS-LONG", strategy.long, comment="long")
strategy.close("SYS-LONG", when=closeLong) //output logic


// Stop Loss 


sl = input(75)
Stop = sl * 10
Q = 100


strategy.exit("Out Long", "SYS-LONG", qty_percent=Q, loss=Stop)
strategy.exit("Out Short", "SYS-SHORT", qty_percent=Q, loss=Stop)

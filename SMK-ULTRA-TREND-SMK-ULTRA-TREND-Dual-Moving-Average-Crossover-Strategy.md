#### Overview
The SMK ULTRA TREND double moving average crossover strategy is a quantitative trading strategy that generates trading signals based on the crossover signals of the 5-day exponential moving average (EMA5) and the 20-day exponential moving average (EMA20). The core idea of ​​this strategy is to use the intersection of the short-term moving average and the medium-term moving average to capture changes in market trends. When EMA5 crosses above EMA20, a buy signal is generated, and when EMA5 crosses below EMA20, a sell signal is generated. At the same time, this strategy also combines the concepts of support and resistance levels, and helps determine the direction and strength of the trend by drawing support and resistance lines on the chart.
#### Strategy Principle
The principle of SMK ULTRA TREND double moving average crossover strategy can be summarized as the following steps:
1. Calculate the 5-day EMA and the 20-day EMA. EMA can respond to price changes faster than the simple moving average (SMA) and is more suitable for capturing short-term trends.
2. Determine the intersection of EMA5 and EMA20. When EMA5 crosses above EMA20, a buy signal is generated; when EMA5 crosses below EMA20, a sell signal is generated.
3. Calculate support and resistance levels. Determine support and resistance levels by identifying the lowest and highest prices over the past 5 trading days.
4. Draw EMA5, EMA20, support and resistance lines on the chart to visually display strategic signals and key price levels.
5. Execute trades based on cross signals. Open a long position when a buy signal appears and close a position when a sell signal appears.

3. Trend tracking: The EMA indicator pays more attention to recent price changes than the SMA, and can reflect changes in price trends in a timely manner, helping to follow the trend.
4. Assisted judgment of support and resistance: The introduction of support and resistance lines can better grasp the strength of the trend and potential timing of market changes, and provide more reference for trading decisions.

2. Hysteresis: As a trend following strategy, the double moving average crossover strategy inevitably has a certain degree of lag, and may miss the best time to start the trend, or delay exiting when the trend reverses.
3. False signals: When the market is noisy, moving average crossovers may produce some false signals, leading to poor strategy performance.

//@version=5
strategy("SMK ULTRA TREND STRATEGY", overlay=true)

// Define the length for EMAs
ema5_length = 5
ema20_length = 20

// Calculate EMAs
ema5 = ta.ema(close, ema5_length)
ema20 = ta.ema(close, ema20_length)

// Plot EMAs
plot(ema5, title="EMA 5", color=color.red )
plot(ema20, title="EMA 20", color=color.blue)

// Generate buy and sell signals
buySignal = ta.crossover(ema5, ema20)
sellSignal = ta.crossunder(ema5, ema20)

// Plot buy and sell signals
plotshape(series=buySignal, location=location.belowbar, color=color.green, style=shape.labelup, text="BUY")
plotshape(series=sellSignal, location=location.abovebar, color=color.red, style=shape.labeldown, text="SELL")

// Execute buy and sell orders
if (buySignal)
    strategy.entry("Buy", strategy.long)
if (sellSignal)
    strategy.close("sell")

// Define support and resistance lengths
pivotLen = 5

// Calculate support and resistance levels
var float supportLevel = na
var float resistanceLevel = na

if (ta.pivotlow(low, pivotLen, pivotLen))
    supportLevel := low[pivotLen]

if (ta.pivothigh(high, pivotLen, pivotLen))
    resistanceLevel := high[pivotLen]

// Plot support and resistance levels
plot(supportLevel, title="Support Level", color=color.green, linewidth=2, style=plot.style_linebr)
plot(resistanceLevel, title="Resistance Level", color=color.red, linewidth=2, style=plot.style_linebr)

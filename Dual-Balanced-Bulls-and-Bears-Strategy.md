## Overview
The dual equilibrium bull-bear strategy is a combination strategy that combines the 123 reversal strategy and the long-short equilibrium indicator. This strategy aims to use the signals generated by the 123 reversal strategy and the signals of the long-short equilibrium indicator for verification to achieve more reliable market entry.
## Principle
This strategy consists of two sub-strategies:
1. 123 reversal strategy. This strategy generates a signal when the last two closing prices reverse, that is, go long if the closing price of the first two days falls and the closing price of the third day rises, and go short if the closing price of the first two days rises and the closing price of the third day falls. At the same time, this strategy will also combine the STOCH indicator and generate a signal only when the STOCH indicator shows oversold or overbought.
2. Long-short equilibrium indicator strategy. This strategy determines the market trend by calculating the balance of bull power and short power. Specifically, it calculates the difference between the closing price and the opening price of the day and the difference between the previous day and the current day to determine the strength of the bulls and the bears. The larger the difference between long and short forces, the clearer the trend.
The trading signals of the combined strategy are derived from the trading signals of the above two sub-strategies. Only when the signals of the two sub-strategies are consistent, for example, when both indicate long, the combined strategy will take the signal to enter the market. If the signals sent by the two sub-strategies are inconsistent, the combined strategy will skip the signal and adopt a wait-and-see attitude.
## Advantages
The biggest advantage of the dual equilibrium bull-bear strategy is its high reliability. Because it requires two sub-strategies to send consistent signals before entering the market, it can serve as a verification and avoid false signals. In addition, the two sub-strategies explore opportunities from two aspects: reversal and trend respectively, which can achieve strategic diversification and avoid the risks of a single strategy.
123 Reversal strategy can capture short-term market reversal opportunities. The long-short equilibrium strategy can determine the long-term trend direction. Using the two together, you can grasp the main trend while reversing, filter out weaker reversal signals, and thereby increase the probability of profit.
## Risk
The biggest risk of this strategy is that the sub-strategy doubles the probability of sending a false signal. Although the combined strategy requires the signals from both parties to be consistent, when the two sub-strategies send wrong signals at the same time, the combined strategy will also enter the market and bear double losses.
In addition, sub-strategies may diverge, with one signaling a long position and another a short position. At this time, the combination strategy will miss the opportunity. If the differences persist, the portfolio strategy may not be able to enter the market for a long time, resulting in a decrease in capital efficiency.

You can consider introducing 추세-역전 전략(trend-reversal strategy) as the third sub-strategy. This strategy can determine long-term trends and generate signals when trends reverse. Adding strategies to judge market trends can help filter out false signals and improve stability.
Another optimization direction is to adjust the parameters of sub-strategies so that they can generate more matching trading signals. For example, the threshold parameters of the long-short equilibrium strategy can be adjusted to capture weaker trends, thereby complementing the reversal strategy.
In addition, ways to deal with persistent divergence of sub-policies can be studied. For example, set the maximum tolerated number of divergences, and take the signal of a separate sub-strategy to enter the market after exceeding it. This can mitigate the loss of opportunity to some extent.
## Summarize
The dual equilibrium bull-bear strategy achieves double verification of trading signals by combining the 123 reversal strategy and the long-short equilibrium strategy, which can effectively filter out false signals and improve stability. At the same time, it combines reversal strategies and trend strategies to achieve diversification of strategies and reduce risks. ## Overview

The Dual Balanced Bulls and Bears strategy is a combination strategy that incorporates the 123 Reversal strategy and Bulls and Bears Balance indicator. It aims to verify signals from the 123 Reversal strategy with the Bulls and Bears Balance indicator for more reliable market entry.

## Principles 

The strategy consists of two sub-strategies:

1. 123 Reversal strategy. It generates signals when the last two closing prices show reversal, i.e. go long when the previous two closing prices were declining while the third closing price rises, and go short when the previous two closing prices were rising while the third closing price declines. It also incorporates the STOCH indicator to only take signals when STOCH shows oversold or overbought conditions.

2. Bulls and Bears Balance indicator strategy. It judges the market trend by calculating the balance between bullish and bearish forces. Specifically, it uses the difference between the current closing price and opening price, as well as the difference between the current and previous day to determine the bullish and bearish forces. The larger the difference between bullish and bearish forces, the more pronounced the trend.

The combined strategy sources its trading signals from the signals generated by the two sub-strategies. It will only take a signal, e.g. go long, when the two sub-strategies give consistent signals, i.e. both signal to go long. If the signals from the two sub-strategies differ, the combined strategy will skip that signal and remain sidelines.

## Advantages

The biggest advantage of the Dual Balanced Bulls and Bears strategy is its high reliability. By requiring consistent signals from both sub-strategies before entering a trade, it serves as a verification mechanism to avoid false signals. In addition, with the two sub-strategies tapping opportunities from reversal and trend sides respectively, the strategy offers diversification to mitigate risks from a single strategy.

The 123 Reversal strategy can capture short-term reversal opportunities in the market. The Bulls and Bears Balance strategy can determine the direction of the longer-term trend. Using both enables capturing reversals while sticking to the major trend, filtering out weaker reversal signals and increasing win rate.

## Risks

The biggest risk is that the probability of wrong signals from the sub-strategies doubles. Although the combined strategy requires consistent signals, if both sub-strategies give wrong signals at the same time, the combined strategy will still enter the trade, incurring doubled losses.

Also, conflicts can arise between the sub-strategies, with one signaling to go long while the other short. The combined strategy will then miss opportunities. Prolonged conflicts may prevent the combined strategy from entering for a long time, decreasing capital efficiency.

## Optimization

Consider incorporating a trend-reversal strategy as a third sub-strategy. It can help determine the longer-term trend and give signals when the trend reverses. Adding a strategy to judge the market trend can further filter out false signals and increase stability.

Another direction is adjusting the parameters of the sub-strategies for more aligned signals. For example, adjusting the threshold parameters of the Bulls and Bears Balance strategy to capture weaker trends and complement the reversal strategy.

Handling prolonged conflicts between the sub-strategies can also be researched. For example, setting a maximum tolerance level for conflicts, after which the signal from an individual sub-strategy will be taken. This can alleviate opportunity loss to some extent.

## Conclusion

The Dual Balanced Bulls and Bears strategy combines the 123 Reversal strategy and Bulls and Bears Balance strategy to achieve dual verification of trading signals and effectively filter out false signals and increase stability. Meanwhile, combining reversal and trend strategies provides diversification to lower risks. The strategy can be further optimized by adjusting parameters, adding a third strategy etc. to improve alignment and capital efficiency. Overall, the strategy has novel ideas and substantial practical value.

Reversal123(Length, KSmoothing, DLength, Level) =>
    vFast = sma(stoch(close, high, low, Length), KSmoothing) 
    vSlow = sma(vFast, DLength)
    pos = 0.0
    pos := iff(close[2] < close[1] and close > close[1] and vFast < vSlow and vFast > Level, 1,
	         iff(close[2] > close[1] and close < close[1] and vFast > vSlow and vFast < Level, -1, nz(pos[1], 0))) 
	pos

BullAndBearBalance(SellLevel, BuyLevel) =>
    pos = 0
    value =  iff (close < open , 
              iff (close[1] > open ,  max(close - open, high - low), high - low), 
               iff (close > open, 
                 iff(close[1] > open, max(close[1] - low, high - close), max(open - low, high - close)), 
                  iff(high - close > close - low, 
                   iff (close[1] > open, max(close[1] - open, high - low), high - low), 
                     iff (high - close < close - low, 
                      iff(close > open, max(close - low, high - close),open - low), 
                       iff (close > open, max(close[1] - open, high - close),
                         iff(close[1] < open, max(open - low, high - close), high - low))))))

              iff (close[1] < open ,  max(high - close[1], close - low), max(high - open, close - low)), 
               iff (close > open, 
                 iff(close[1] > open,  high - low, max(open - close[1], high - low)),

                         iff(close[1] < open, max(open - close, high - low), high - low))))))
    nBBB = value2 - value
    pos := iff(nBBB < SellLevel, -1,
    	   iff(nBBB >= BuyLevel, 1, nz(pos[1], 0))) 
    pos

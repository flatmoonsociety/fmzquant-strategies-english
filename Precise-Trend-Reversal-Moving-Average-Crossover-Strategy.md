## Overview
This strategy is called "Golden Cross Death Cross Strategy". Its main idea is to use two powerful technical indicator signals, golden cross and death cross, of two moving averages with different periods to capture the reversal of the market trend and achieve the effect of buying low and selling high.
## Strategy Principle
In this strategy, we calculate 50-period and 200-period simple moving averages (SMA). According to traditional understanding, when the 50-day moving average falls from above and crosses the 200-day moving average, it is called a "death cross" and is a bearish signal. When the 50-day line breaks through the 200-day line from below, it is called a "golden cross" and is a bullish signal.
The trading logic of this strategy is to establish positions based on the occurrence of these two signals. Specifically, the strategy will go short when a "death cross" occurs and go long when a "golden cross" occurs. This way you can make profits near turning points in the market trend.
In addition, a customizable backtesting time frame function is provided in the strategy. This allows us to test strategy performance over different date ranges to discover the actual effects of these crossover signals.
## Strategic Advantages
1. It can effectively capture the reversal point of the market trend and open positions near key points to make profits.
2. The cross combination of two moving averages with different periods is used to avoid false signals.
3. Provide backtesting function to test the actual performance of the strategy under different market conditions
4. The drawing is clear, and cross signals and position changes can be visually seen
## Strategy Risk
1. There is a lag in the moving average crossover signal and it is impossible to predict the reversal of extreme market conditions.
2. Backtest data may be different from actual data, and actual performance will be limited by transaction costs and slippage.
3. The choice of strategy parameters such as the moving average period will have a great impact on the results.
4. Pay attention to the fundamental situation and technical form, and do not trade mechanically
For risks, we can adjust the moving average parameters, filter signals in combination with other indicators, manage funds well, and verify the strategy with real offers to reduce actual risks.

The strategy logic is simple and clear, and it also provides convenient backtesting functions. We can use it as an integral part of trend tracking to assist in judging market conditions. The trading logic is simply to take positions based on these signals - shorting at death cross and going long at golden cross. This allows us to profit around inflection points when market trend reverses.

``` pinescript
/*backtest
start: 2024-01-14 00:00:00
end: 2024-01-21 00:00:00
period: 15m
basePeriod: 5m
exchanges: [{"eid":"Futures_Binance","currency":"BTC_USDT"}]
*/

//@version=5
strategy("[S_R__9] - Death and Golden Cross", overlay=true)

// Specific Time Date Range For Backtest
startDate = input.int(title='Start Date', defval=1, minval=1, maxval=31, group='DATE CONFIG')
startMonth = input.int(title='Start Month', defval=1, minval=1, maxval=12, group='DATE CONFIG')
startYear = input.int(title='Start Year', defval=2023, minval=1800, maxval=2100, group='DATE CONFIG')

endDate = input.int(title='End Date', defval=31, minval=1, maxval=31, group='DATE CONFIG')
endMonth = input.int(title='End Month', defval=12, minval=1, maxval=12, group='DATE CONFIG')
endYear = input.int(title='End Year', defval=2023, minval=1800, maxval=2100, group='DATE CONFIG')

SPECIFIC_DATE = input.bool(title='USE SPECIFIC DATE ?', defval=false, group='DATE CONFIG')

inDateRange = SPECIFIC_DATE ? time >= timestamp(syminfo.timezone, startYear, startMonth, startDate, 0, 0) and time < timestamp(syminfo.timezone, endYear, endMonth, endDate, 0, 0) : true

// Calculate 50 SMA and 200 SMA
sma50 = ta.sma(close, 50)
sma200 = ta.sma(close, 200)

// Detect a Death Cross (50 SMA crossing below 200 SMA)
deathCross = ta.crossunder(sma50, sma200)
// Detect a Golden Cross (50 SMA crossing above 200 SMA)
goldenCross = ta.crossover(sma50, sma200)

// Strategy Execution
if (inDateRange)
    if (deathCross)
        strategy.entry("Death Cross long", strategy.short)

    if (goldenCross)
        strategy.entry("Golden Cross short", strategy.long)

// Plot SMAs
plot(sma50, color=color.red, title="50 SMA")
plot(sma200, color=color.blue, title="200 SMA")

// Plotting Death Cross signal
plotshape(series=deathCross and inDateRange, title="Death Cross Signal", location=location.belowbar, color=color.red, style=shape.labeldown, text="DEATH CROSS")

// Plotting Golden Cross signal
plotshape(series=goldenCross and inDateRange, title="Golden Cross Signal", location=location.abovebar, color=color.green, style=shape.labelup, text="GOLDEN CROSS")

```

### Overview
This is a strategy that uses multiple technical indicators to determine trading signals. It integrates four mainstream technical indicators including the double moving average crossover system, weighted moving average, MACD and TSI of the Turtle Trading Rules to form a multi-confirmation trading strategy. This combination can effectively filter false signals and improve stability.
### Principle
The core principle of this strategy is the cross combination of multiple technical indicators. Including the following aspects:
1. Use the double moving average crossover of the Turtle trading rule to generate trading signals. Calculate the 7-day and 14-day double Hull moving averages respectively. When the short-term moving average crosses the long-term moving average, it is bullish, and when it crosses below, it is bearish.
2. Calculate the 1-day weighted moving average as an important long-term trend judgment indicator.
3. Calculate the MACD indicator and determine the golden cross between it and the signal line. When the MACD is greater than the signal line, it is bullish, and when it is less than the signal line, it is bearish.
4. Calculate the TSI indicator and determine whether it is above the overbought line or below the oversold line. TSI is bearish when it is above the overbought line and bullish when it is below the oversold line.

When entering, you must meet the following conditions at the same time:
- The 7-day line crosses the 14-day line
- If the 1-day weighted moving average is below, only go long; if it is above, only go short
- MACD crosses above the signal line
- TSI is higher than the oversold line (go long) or lower than the overbought line (short)
This can effectively avoid false signals generated by a single technical indicator and improve stability.

This multi-indicator cross combination strategy has the following advantages:
1. Multiple confirmations to effectively filter out false signals and avoid erroneous transactions.
2. Technical indicators cover the short, medium and long term and can capture trading opportunities at different levels.

5. The TSI indicator is relatively smooth and can effectively identify overbought and oversold conditions.
6. As an important long-term trend indicator, the moving average prevents counter-trend trading.
To sum up, this strategy combines the advantages of multiple indicators, is both stable and flexible, and has large profit potential. It is an excellent quantitative strategy.

This strategy also has certain risks, mainly focusing on the following aspects:
1. Multiple indicators increase the complexity of the strategy, making parameter setting and optimization difficult.
2. There may be differences between indicators, thus affecting the stability of the strategy.
3. The probability of technical indicators sending false signals cannot be completely eliminated.
4. Miss the opportunity of short-term market reversal and fail to capture the arbitrage space brought by the rapid reversal.
Correspondingly, further optimization can be achieved from the following aspects:

//@version=2
//                                                    Quad-HullMA-cross & VWMA & MacD & TSI combination  <<<<< by SeaSide420 >>>>>>
strategy("MultiCross", overlay=true)
keh=input(title="Double HullMA 1",defval=7, minval=1)
teh=input(title="Double HullMA 2",defval=14, minval=1)
meh=input(title="VWMA",defval=1, minval=1)
meh1=vwma(close,round(meh))
n2ma=2*wma(close,round(keh/2))
nma=wma(close,keh)
diff=n2ma-nma,sqn=round(sqrt(keh))
n2ma1=2*wma(close[2],round(keh/2))
nma1=wma(close[2],keh)
diff1=n2ma1-nma1,sqn1=round(sqrt(keh))
n1=wma(diff,sqn)
n2=wma(diff1,sqn)
b=n1>n2?lime:red
c=n1>n2?green:red
n2ma3=2*wma(close,round(teh/2))
nma2=wma(close,teh)
diff2=n2ma3-nma2,sqn2=round(sqrt(teh))
n2ma4=2*wma(close[2],round(teh/2))
nma3=wma(close[2],teh)
diff3=n2ma4-nma3,sqn3=round(sqrt(teh))
n3=wma(diff2,sqn2)
n4=wma(diff3,sqn3)
fastLength = input(title="MacD fastLength", defval=7)
slowlength = input(title="MacD slowlength", defval=14)
MACDLength = input(title="MacD Length", defval=3)
MACD = ema(close, fastLength) - ema(close, slowlength)
aMACD = ema(MACD, MACDLength)
delta = MACD - aMACD
a1=plot(n1,color=c),a2=plot(n2,color=c)
plot(cross(n1, n2) ? n1 : na, style = cross, color=b, linewidth = 3)
a3=plot(n3,color=c),a4=plot(n4,color=c)
plot(cross(n3, n4) ? n1 : na, style = cross, color=b, linewidth = 3)
//a5=plot(meh1,color=c)
long = input(title="TSI Long Length",  defval=5)
short = input(title="TSI Short Length",  defval=3)
signal = input(title="TSI Signal Length",  defval=2)
linebuy = input(title="TSI Upper Line",  defval=4)
linesell = input(title="TSI Lower Line",  defval=-4)
price = close
double_smooth(src, long, short) =>
    fist_smooth = ema(src, long)
    ema(fist_smooth, short)
pc = change(price)
double_smoothed_pc = double_smooth(pc, long, short)
double_smoothed_abs_pc = double_smooth(abs(pc), long, short)
tsi_value = 100 * (double_smoothed_pc / double_smoothed_abs_pc)
closelong = n1<n2 and n3<n4 and n1>meh1
if (closelong)
    strategy.close("Long")
closeshort = n1>n2 and n3>n4 and n1<meh1
if (closeshort)
    strategy.close("Short") 
longCondition = strategy.opentrades<1 and n1>n2 and MACD>aMACD and n1<meh1 and n3>n4 and ema(tsi_value, signal)>linesell
if (longCondition)
    strategy.entry("Long",strategy.long)
shortCondition = strategy.opentrades<1  and n1<n2 and MACD<aMACD and n1>meh1 and n3<n4 and ema(tsi_value, signal)<linebuy
if (shortCondition)
    strategy.entry("Short",strategy.short)

## Overview
This strategy combines the 123 reversal strategy and the linear regression intercept strategy to achieve a multi-factor driven combined trading strategy. The 123 reversal strategy determines the price relationship between the last two trading days and combines the Stoch indicator to determine reversal signals. The linear regression intercept strategy uses linear regression analysis to determine the relationship between price and trend lines to generate trading signals. The two strategies verify each other and can effectively filter false signals.
## Strategy Principle
### 123 Reversal Strategy
This strategy is based on the following principles:
1. If the relationship between the closing prices of the last two trading days is that today's closing price is higher than yesterday's, and the Stoch fast line is lower than the slow line, it is considered that there is a bullish reversal signal.
2. If the relationship between the closing prices of the last two trading days is that today's closing price is lower than yesterday's, and the Stoch fast line is higher than the slow line, it is considered that there is a bearish reversal signal.
The judgment rules are as follows:
- If today's closing price > yesterday's closing price and Stoch fast line < Stoch slow line and Stoch fast line > set parameters, a buy signal will be generated
- If today's closing price < yesterday's closing price and Stoch fast line > Stoch slow line and Stoch fast line < set parameters, a sell signal will be generated
This strategy requires setting the Stoch indicator parameters, including: calculating Stoch's K-line period Length, Stoch fast line smoothing period KSmoothing, Stoch slow line smoothing period DLength, and Stoch fast line judgment threshold Level.
### Linear regression intercept strategy
This strategy is based on linear regression analysis and determines the relationship between price and linear regression trend line. The judgment rules are as follows:
- If the closing price is greater than the linear regression intercept, a buy signal is generated
- If the closing price is less than the linear regression intercept, a sell signal is generated
This strategy requires setting the linear regression period LengthLRI, and the linear regression input data source xSeria.
### Combination strategy
This combined strategy needs to meet the buy/sell signals of both the 123 reversal strategy and the linear regression intercept strategy before it can generate actual trading instructions, thereby effectively filtering out false signals and improving trading results.
## Advantage Analysis
This strategy has the following advantages:
1. Multi-factor drive, effectively filter out false signals and improve signal quality
Combining two different types of strategies, both strategies must generate signals at the same time before an order can actually be placed. This multi-factor verification mechanism can filter out erroneous signals occasionally generated by a certain strategy, reduce unnecessary transactions, and effectively improve signal quality.
2. Monitor the relationship between price and trend in real time to avoid being trapped
The linear regression intercept can reflect the relationship between price and trend line in real time. If the price has seriously deviated from the trend, it will promptly prompt the strategy to adjust the position direction. This way you can stop losses in time and avoid being stuck in the historical trend.
3. Consider both trend and reversal trading opportunities
Linear regression strategies are better at identifying trend buying and selling points. The 123 reversal strategy focuses on reversal point identification. The two strategies can combine the advantages of trend trading and reversal trading very well.
4. Strategy parameters can be customized and optimized.
Both strategies provide certain parameters for customization. Parameters can be optimized for different varieties and different trends to optimize the effect of the combined strategy.
## Risk Analysis
This strategy also has the following risks:
1. Multi-factor driving may miss some opportunities
Trading signals that must satisfy two strategies will miss some opportunities to make profits by relying only on a single strategy. If the effect of a certain strategy is weakened, it will drag down the overall trading effect.
2. Linear regression has hysteresis
Linear regression requires certain historical data for calculation, cannot respond to emergencies in real time, and has a certain lag. If there is a large gap in price, the linear regression trend line will take a certain amount of time to adjust, and false signals may be generated during this time.
3. Reasonable parameter optimization is required
Both strategies require the selection of appropriate parameters, and parameters may need to be adjusted independently for some varieties. If the parameters are selected improperly, the effect of the strategy will be greatly reduced.
The corresponding risks can be reduced through the following methods:
1. Appropriately relax the trigger conditions of combined signals to prevent missing too many opportunities
2. Combine with trend indicators and other alternative linear regressions to obtain more real-time trend judgments
3. Use machine learning and other methods to assist parameter optimization and improve parameter selection effects.

Reversal123(Length, KSmoothing, DLength, Level) =>
    vFast = sma(stoch(close, high, low, Length), KSmoothing) 
    vSlow = sma(vFast, DLength)
    pos = 0.0
    pos := iff(close[2] < close[1] and close > close[1] and vFast < vSlow and vFast > Level, 1,
	         iff(close[2] > close[1] and close < close[1] and vFast > vSlow and vFast < Level, -1, nz(pos[1], 0))) 
	pos

LRI(Length,xSeria) =>
    pos = 0.0
    xX = Length * (Length - 1) * 0.5
    xDivisor = xX * xX - Length * Length * (Length - 1) * (2 * Length - 1) / 6
    xXY = 0.0
    for i = 0 to Length-1
    	xXY := xXY + (i * xSeria[i])
    xSlope = (Length * xXY - xX * sum(xSeria, Length)) / xDivisor
    xLRI = (sum(xSeria, Length) - xSlope * xX) / Length
    pos:= iff(close > xLRI, 1,
           iff(close < xLRI, -1, nz(pos[1], 0))) 
    pos

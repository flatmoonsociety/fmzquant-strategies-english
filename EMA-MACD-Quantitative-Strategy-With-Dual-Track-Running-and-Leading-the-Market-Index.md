## Overview
This strategy mainly uses the EMA moving average and MACD indicators to judge changes in market classification and perform operations of chasing ups and downs. The core idea is to go long when the short-term EMA line breaks through the long-term EMA line from below and the MACD crosses the 0-axis upward at the same time; when the short-term EMA breaks through the long-term EMA from above and the MACD crosses the 0-axis downward at the same time, go short.
## Principle
This strategy combines the Moving Average indicator and the MACD indicator.
First of all, it uses two EMA indicators with different length periods, one is the 25-period EMA line and the other is the 50-period EMA line. The 25-period EMA line can reflect short-term trends, and the 50-period EMA line can reflect mid- and long-term trends. When the short-term EMA line crosses the long-term EMA line from below, it means that the market has turned from falling to rising, which is a golden cross signal and is a long-term judgment. When the short-term EMA crosses the long-term EMA from above, it means that the market has turned from rising to falling, which is a dead cross signal and is a short-selling judgment.
Secondly, this strategy also combines the MACD indicator to determine signals. The MACD indicator includes the DIF line and the DEA line, which represent the difference between the short-term and long-term exponential smoothing moving averages, calculated through double EMA. This strategy sets DIF to be the 12-day EMA minus the 26-day EMA. The DEA line is the 9-day exponential moving average of DIF. The DIF line represents momentum, and the DEA line represents the average of MACD. A buy signal is generated when DIF crosses the DEA line from below. A sell signal is generated when DIF crosses DEA from above.
Combining these two indicators, when the 25-day EMA crosses the 50-day EMA and the DIF line of MACD crosses the DEA line at the same time, a buy signal is generated to go long; when the 25-day EMA crosses the 50-day EMA and the DIF line of MACD crosses the DEA line below, a sell signal is generated to go short.
## Advantage Analysis
This is a very typical dual-track strategy that combines with the MACD indicator to produce more reliable trading signals and has the following advantages:

3. Using the 25-day and 50-day lines as the fast and slow lines, the parameter selection is more accurate and can capture the obvious trend changes in the medium and short term.
4. By using the idea of ​​chasing the rise and killing the fall, you can outperform the market index and obtain greater profits when the market rises and falls sharply.
5. The strategy logic is simple and clear, easy to understand and implement, and is suitable for quantitative beginners.
6. Parameters can be carefully and appropriately optimized to better adapt the strategy to different varieties and market environments.
## Risk Analysis
This strategy also has some risks that you need to be wary of:
1. The possibility of false signals generated by the EMA moving average still exists. In case of violent market conditions, the whipsaw phenomenon will still occur.
2. The MACD indicator parameters need to be constantly optimized and adjusted, otherwise false signals or signal lags will occur.
3. You need to be vigilant about whether the stop loss point is set appropriately to avoid invalid breakthroughs causing large losses.
4. It is necessary to pay attention to changes in the market and policy environment to avoid large losses caused by systemic risks.

``` pinescript
/*backtest
start: 2022-12-05 00:00:00
end: 2023-12-11 00:00:00

//@version=4
strategy(title="EMA+MACD", shorttitle="EMA+MACD", overlay=true)
// Getting inputs
fast_length = input(title="Fast Length", type=input.integer, defval=12)
slow_length = input(title="Slow Length", type=input.integer, defval=26)
src = input(title="Source", type=input.source, defval=close)


signal_length = input(title="Signal Smoothing", type=input.integer, minval = 1, maxval = 50, defval = 9)
sma_source = input(title="Oscillator MA Type", type=input.string, defval="EMA", options=["SMA", "EMA"])
sma_signal = input(title="Signal Line MA Type", type=input.string, defval="EMA", options=["SMA", "EMA"])

fast_ma = sma_source == "SMA" ? sma(src, fast_length) : ema(src, fast_length)
slow_ma = sma_source == "SMA" ? sma(src, slow_length) : ema(src, slow_length)
macd = fast_ma - slow_ma
signal = sma_signal == "SMA" ? sma(macd, signal_length) : ema(macd, signal_length)
hist = macd - signal
len1 = input(title="Len Ema 1 ",type=input.integer,defval=25)
len2 = input(title="Len Ema 2 ",type=input.integer,defval=50)
ema1 = ema(src,len1)
ema2 = ema(src,len2)

bull = crossover(ema1,ema2) and macd > 0
bear = crossover(ema2,ema1) and macd < 0
l1 = bull ? label.new(x=bar_index,y=low,yloc=yloc.belowbar,text="BUY",color=color.green,textcolor=color.white,style=label.style_triangleup) : na
l2 = bear ? label.new(x=bar_index,y=high,yloc=yloc.abovebar,text="SELL",color=color.red,textcolor=color.white,style=label.style_triangledown) : na


strategy.entry("LONG",strategy.long,when=bull)
strategy.entry("SHORT",strategy.short,when=bear)



```

### Overview
This strategy uses the moving average as the main technical indicator and combines the RSI indicator as the filter condition to implement a relatively simple trend following strategy. A trading signal is generated when the price falls below or exceeds the moving average for a specified period. At the same time, the RSI indicator can be used to determine overbuying or selling to avoid wrong transactions. Generally speaking, this strategy is suitable for tracking medium and long-term trends, and can obtain better returns in strong trends.
### Strategy Principles
This strategy is mainly based on moving averages and RSI indicators. Moving averages are widely used to determine price trend direction and strength. When the price is above the moving average, it indicates that the current trend is up; when the price is below the moving average, it indicates that the current trend is down. Therefore, price breaks above the moving average can be used as a basis for generating trading signals. On the other hand, the RSI indicator can be used to determine whether the market is overbought or oversold. An RSI above 70 indicates it may be overbought, and an RSI below 30 indicates it may be oversold. Therefore, this strategy uses the RSI indicator to filter the trading signals generated by the moving average. Only when the RSI indicator shows that there is no overbought or oversold, real trading instructions will be generated.
Specifically, a buy signal is generated when the price is below the moving average and the RSI is below 30; a sell signal is generated when the price is above the moving average and the RSI is above 70. Open a long or short position based on these trading signals.
### Advantage Analysis
This strategy has the following advantages:
1. Simple operation and easy to implement. It mainly relies on moving average indicators and does not have high technical requirements for traders.
2. Able to effectively track price trends, especially suitable for medium and long-term operations.
3. The application of RSI indicator can avoid unnecessary wrong transactions and filter out false signals.
4. There is no need to adjust parameters frequently, which reduces the risk of over-optimization.
5. High scalability, more indicators or optimization rules can be introduced to improve it.
### Risk Analysis
This strategy also has the following risks:
1. Within the price fluctuation range, more false signals will be generated, leading to losses.
2. Failure to judge the trend reversal point well may result in losses due to wrong positions being established before and after the market turning point.
3. Improper parameter settings (such as moving average period length) may affect strategy performance.
4. Unable to adapt to violent market conditions caused by emergencies.
5. Backtest data fitting risks, the actual performance may be different from the backtest results.
### Optimization direction
This strategy can be optimized from the following aspects:
1. Add a stop loss mechanism. You can set a trailing stop or a thickness stop to control the risk of a single loss.
2. Add trend judgment indicators. For example, MACD, KD, etc. can help determine the trend direction and avoid generating false signals.
3. Optimize moving average parameters. The impact of different cycle parameters on strategy stability and profitability can be tested.
4. Add transaction frequency control. For example, only trade during certain time periods or only during large price movements.
5. Introduce machine learning technology for strategy optimization and training.
### Summarize
Overall, this strategy is a relatively simple and practical trend following strategy. It uses moving averages to determine price trends and directions, while using the RSI indicator to filter out false signals. The main advantages of the strategy are simple operation, easy implementation, and suitable for medium and long-term transactions. The disadvantage is that it cannot handle price shocks and trend reversals well. The future optimization space lies in adding a stop-loss mechanism, introducing more auxiliary indicators to judge trends, and optimizing parameters.

``` pinescript
/*backtest
start: 2024-01-26 00:00:00
end: 2024-02-25 00:00:00
period: 1h

exchanges: [{"eid":"Futures_Binance","currency":"BTC_USDT"}]

//@version=5
strategy("Verbesserte VWAP Strategie mit RSI Filter", overlay=true)

// Eingabeparameter
length = input(5, title="VWAP Länge")
multiplier = input(3.0, title="Standardabweichungs-Multiplikator")
smaLength = input(25, title="SMA Länge für Trendfilter")
rsiPeriod = input(8, title="RSI Periode")
rsiOverbought = input(70, title="RSI Überkauft-Schwelle")
rsiOversold = input(30, title="RSI Überverkauft-Schwelle")

// VWAP, Standardabweichung und RSI
vwapValue = ta.vwap(hlc3, length)
rsi = ta.rsi(close, rsiPeriod)

// Signale mit RSI Filter
buySignal = close < vwapValue and rsi < rsiOversold
sellSignal = close > vwapValue and rsi > rsiOverbought

// Strategie-Logik
if (buySignal)
    strategy.entry("Buy", strategy.long)

if (sellSignal)
    strategy.entry("Sell", strategy.short)

// Zeichnen
plot(vwapValue, color=color.blue, title="VWAP")
hline(rsiOverbought, "RSI Überkauft", color=color.red)
hline(rsiOversold, "RSI Überverkauft", color=color.green)

```

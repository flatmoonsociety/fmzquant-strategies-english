## Strategy Overview
The Bollinger Bands and Relative Strength Index (RSI) combination strategy is a technical analysis strategy that combines two popular technical indicators: Bollinger Bands and RSI, for making entry and exit decisions in the market. This strategy uses price breakouts above and below the Bollinger Bands and overbought and oversold signals from the RSI indicator to identify trading opportunities.
## Strategy Principle
This strategy uses two technical indicators, Bollinger Bands and RSI, to generate trading signals:
1. Bollinger Bands consists of three lines: the middle track (moving average), the upper track (the middle track plus the standard deviation), and the lower track (the middle track minus the standard deviation). When the price breaks through the upper or lower Bollinger Band, a trading signal is generated.
2. RSI measures the speed and magnitude of price changes and is calculated by comparing the ratio of rising days to falling days over a period of time. RSI is used to filter the trading signals generated by Bollinger Bands: only go long when the RSI is below the oversold level, and go short only when the RSI is above the overbought level.
Specifically, the trading signals for this strategy are as follows:
- Long signal: When the price breaks through the lower Bollinger Band downwards and the RSI is below the oversold level, open a long position.
- Short signal: When the price breaks above the upper Bollinger Band and RSI is above the overbought level, open a short position.
- Close the position: Close the position when the price breaks through the Bollinger Band in the opposite direction.
## Strategic Advantages
1. Combining two widely used and recognized technical indicators, the strategy logic is simple and clear.
2. Use RSI to filter trading signals generated by Bollinger Bands, which improves the quality of trading decisions and reduces misleading signals.
3. Strategy parameters can be optimized according to different market characteristics and trading styles, and have certain flexibility and adaptability.
## Strategy Risk
1. Like all trading strategies, this strategy may not perform well in certain market environments, such as when the trend is not obvious or the volatility is extremely low.
2. The selection of strategy parameters has an important impact on strategy performance. Inappropriate parameters may lead to a large number of erroneous trading signals.
3. This strategy does not consider the fundamental factors of the market and relies entirely on price behavior, which may fail in certain event-driven market environments.
## Optimization direction
1. Combine with other confirmation indicators, such as trading volume, trend indicators, etc., to further filter trading signals and improve signal quality.
2. Introduce stop-loss and take-profit mechanisms to control single transaction risks and profit targets, and improve the risk-return characteristics of the strategy.
3. Optimize the strategy parameters, such as the Bollinger Band cycle, deviation multiple, RSI cycle, overbought and oversold thresholds, etc., to find the parameter combination that is most suitable for the current market.
4. Consider the performance under different market conditions, such as trending markets, volatile markets, etc., and adopt different strategy parameters or rules for different markets.
## Summarize
The Bollinger Bands and RSI combination strategy is a simple and practical technical trading strategy that combines the two classic indicators of Bollinger Bands and RSI to produce relatively reliable trading signals. The advantage of this strategy is that the logic is clear and easy to understand and implement. At the same time, the RSI indicator is used to filter the Bollinger Band signal, improving the signal quality. However, this strategy also has some limitations, such as insufficient adaptability to the market environment and lack of consideration of fundamental factors. Therefore, in practical applications, the strategy needs to be optimized and improved based on specific market characteristics and trading styles, such as combining other technical indicators, introducing risk control measures, optimizing parameter selection, etc. In general, the Bollinger Bands and RSI combination strategy provides technical traders with a trading idea and framework for reference, but the success of the strategy also depends on the trader's understanding of the market and accumulation of experience.

> Source (PineScript)

``` pinescript
/*backtest
start: 2023-03-15 00:00:00
end: 2023-10-26 00:00:00
period: 1d
basePeriod: 1h
exchanges: [{"eid":"Futures_Binance","currency":"BTC_USDT"}]
*/

//@version=5
strategy("Bollinger Bands & RSI Strategy", overlay=true)

// Bollinger Bands Parameters
source = close
length = input.int(20, minval=1)
mult = input.float(2.0, minval=0.001, maxval=50)

// RSI Parameters
rsi_length = input.int(14, minval=1)
rsi_oversold = input.int(30, minval=1, maxval=100)
rsi_overbought = input.int(70, minval=1, maxval=100)

// Strategy Entry
basis = ta.sma(source, length)
dev = mult * ta.stdev(source, length)
upper = basis + dev
lower = basis - dev

rsi = ta.rsi(source, rsi_length)

if (ta.crossover(source, lower) and rsi < rsi_oversold)
    strategy.entry("BBandLE", strategy.long, comment="BBandLE")
else
    strategy.cancel(id="BBandLE")

if (ta.crossunder(source, upper) and rsi > rsi_overbought)
    strategy.entry("BBandSE", strategy.short, comment="BBandSE")
else
    strategy.cancel(id="BBandSE")

```

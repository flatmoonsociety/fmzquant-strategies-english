## Overview
This strategy combines two technical indicators, Bollinger Bands and Exponential Moving Average (EMA), to capture market trend opportunities. The main idea of ​​the strategy is to use Bollinger Bands to determine whether the price is at a relatively high or low level, while using EMA as a filter condition for the trend, and finally make trading decisions based on certain logical rules.
## Strategy Principle
1. Calculate Bollinger Bands: By calculating the simple moving average (SMA) and standard deviation of the closing price, the upper and lower tracks of the Bollinger Bands are obtained. The upper track is the SMA plus a certain multiple of the standard deviation, and the lower track is the SMA minus a certain multiple of the standard deviation.
2. Calculate EMA: Calculate the exponential moving average of the closing price based on the set EMA period.
3. Calculate ATR: Calculate the average true fluctuation range (ATR) based on the set ATR period.
4. Buying conditions: When the closing price is higher than both the EMA and the upper Bollinger Band, the buying signal is triggered.
5. Sell conditions: When the closing price falls below the lower Bollinger Band or EMA, a sell signal is triggered.
6. Execute transactions: Execute corresponding long or short transactions according to the buying and selling conditions.
7. Drawing: Draw EMA and Bollinger Bands on the main chart, and draw ATR on the secondary chart.
## Advantage Analysis
1. Bollinger Bands can effectively capture the fluctuation range of prices and help determine whether prices are at a relatively high or low level.
2. EMA can reflect the trend direction of prices and can be used to filter trading signals generated by Bollinger Bands to improve the accuracy of trading.
3. ATR can measure market volatility and provide a reference for trading decisions.
4. The strategy logic is clear and easy to understand and implement.
5. By adjusting the parameters of Bollinger Bands and EMA, you can adapt to different market environments and trading varieties.
## Risk Analysis
1. In volatile markets or trend reversals, this strategy may produce more false signals, leading to frequent trading and losses.
2. The strategy is more sensitive to parameter selection, and different parameter settings may lead to different trading results.
3. The strategy does not consider transaction costs and slippage, which may affect the returns of the strategy in actual transactions.
4. The strategy lacks risk management, such as stop loss and position control.
## Optimization direction
1. Introduce trend confirmation indicators, such as MACD, DMI, etc., to further verify the reliability of the trend and reduce false signals.
2. Optimize parameter selection. You can test different parameter combinations through historical data to find the optimal parameter settings.
3. Add risk management measures, such as setting dynamic stop loss based on ATR, or adjusting position size based on market volatility.
4. Consider the impact of transaction costs and slippage in backtesting and real trading to improve the practicality of the strategy.
5. Combine with other technical indicators or fundamental factors to construct a more comprehensive and robust trading strategy.
## Summarize
The Bollinger Bands + EMA trend tracking strategy combines the two technical indicators of Bollinger Bands and EMA to capture market trend opportunities. The advantage of this strategy is that its logic is clear, easy to understand and implement, and it can adapt to different market environments by adjusting parameters. However, the strategy also has some risks, such as it may generate more false signals during market shocks or trend reversals, is sensitive to parameter selection, and lacks risk management measures. In order to further optimize the strategy, you can consider introducing other trend confirmation indicators, optimizing parameter selection, adding risk management measures, considering the impact of transaction costs and slippage, and combining other technical indicators or fundamental factors. In general, this strategy provides a basic framework for trend trading, but in practical applications it needs to be optimized and improved according to specific circumstances to improve the robustness and profitability of the strategy.

``` pinescript
/*backtest

//@version=4
strategy("Bollinger Bands + EMA Strategy", overlay=true)

// Bollinger Bands settings
bollinger_period = 50
bollinger_width = 2.0

// EMA settings
ema_period = 100

// ATR settings
atr_period = 14
atr_factor = 1.8

// Calculate Bollinger Bands
sma_source = sma(close, bollinger_period)
std_dev = stdev(close, bollinger_period)
upper_band = sma_source + bollinger_width * std_dev
lower_band = sma_source - bollinger_width * std_dev

// Calculate EMA
ema_value = ema(close, ema_period)

// Calculate ATR
atr_value = atr(atr_period)

// Buy condition
buy_condition = close > ema_value and close > upper_band

// Sell condition
sell_condition = crossunder(close, lower_band) or crossunder(close, ema_value)

// Plotting Bollinger Bands and EMA
plot(ema_value, color=color.blue, title="EMA")
plot(upper_band, color=color.green, title="Upper Bollinger Band")
plot(lower_band, color=color.red, title="Lower Bollinger Band")

// Execute orders based on conditions
if (buy_condition)
    strategy.entry("Buy", strategy.long)
if (sell_condition)
    strategy.entry("Sell", strategy.short)

// Plot ATR on separate pane
plot(atr_value, color=color.orange, title="ATR", style=plot.style_stepline, linewidth=1, transp=0)

```

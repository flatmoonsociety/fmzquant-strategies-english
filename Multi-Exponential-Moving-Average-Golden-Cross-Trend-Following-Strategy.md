#### Overview
This strategy is a trend following system based on multiple exponential moving average (EMA) crossover signals. By combining the 20, 50 and 150 period EMAs, a complete trend identification and trade execution framework is constructed. The strategy uses the cross relationship between EMAs of different periods to determine changes in market trends and specific trading opportunities.
#### Strategy Principle
The strategy uses three exponential moving averages with different periods: EMA20 for short-term trends, EMA50 for mid-term trends, and EMA150 for long-term trends. When EMA50 crosses above EMA150, a golden cross is formed, indicating the formation of a long-term upward trend; when EMA50 crosses below EMA150, a death cross is formed, indicating the formation of a long-term downward trend. Specific trading signals are generated by the intersection of EMA20 and EMA50: when EMA20 crosses above EMA50, a buy signal is generated, and when EMA20 crosses below EMA50, a sell signal is generated.
#### Strategic Advantages
1. Strong signal stability: By using multiple moving average filtering, false signals are effectively reduced.
2. Accurate trend grasp: Combining short, medium and long-term trends, it can judge the market direction more accurately.
3. Improved risk control: timely close positions based on trend reversal to avoid sharp retracements.
4. There is plenty of room for parameter optimization: the moving average cycle can be adjusted according to different market characteristics.
5. Clear execution logic: The trading rules are simple and clear, easy to understand and execute.
#### Strategy Risk
1. Trend reversal lag: Moving averages are essentially lagging indicators and may cause certain losses at trend turning points.
2. Poor performance in volatile markets: In sideways volatile markets, frequent crossovers may lead to over-trading.
3. Parameter sensitivity: The selection of different period parameters will significantly affect the strategy performance.
4. Market adaptability: The strategy performs better in strong trending markets, but may not be effective in other market environments.
#### Strategy optimization direction
1. Add trend strength filtering: Trend strength indicators such as ADX can be introduced to filter trading signals in weak trend environments.
2. Optimize the stop-loss mechanism: Design a dynamic stop-loss plan, such as a volatility stop-loss based on ATR.
3. Introduce volatility adaptation: dynamically adjust EMA parameters according to market volatility to improve strategy adaptability.
4. Improve position management: Design a dynamic position management system based on trend strength.
5. Increase market environment judgment: judge the market status based on indicators such as trading volume and volatility, and selectively launch strategies.

// EMAs
ema20 = ta.ema(close, 20)
ema50 = ta.ema(close, 50)
ema150 = ta.ema(close, 150)

// Cross conditions

flatCondition = ta.crossunder(ema20, ema50)
deathCross = ta.crossunder(ema50, ema150)
goldenCross = ta.crossover(ema50, ema150)

plotshape(series=goldenCross, location=location.belowbar, color=color.green, style=shape.labelup, title="Golden Cross", size=size.small, text="Golden Cross")

plotshape(series=flatCondition, location=location.abovebar, color=color.orange, style=shape.triangledown, title="Sell Signal", size=size.small, text="Sell")

// Trade execution
if longCondition and strategy.position_size == 0
    strategy.entry("Long", strategy.long)

if flatCondition and strategy.position_size > 0
    strategy.close("Long")

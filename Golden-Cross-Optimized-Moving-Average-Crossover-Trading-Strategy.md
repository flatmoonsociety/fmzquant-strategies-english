## Overview
This strategy optimizes the conventional moving average crossover strategy, sets three moving averages of different periods, and uses 9-period, 50-period, and 100-period moving averages to construct a golden cross shape. When the medium and long-term moving averages are in an upward trend, the short-term moving average crosses the mid-line moving average to form a golden cross buying signal. The strategy name is "Moving Average Cross Golden Cross Optimization Trading Strategy".
## Strategy Principle
This strategy uses three moving averages of 9, 50 and 100 periods. Among them, the 9-period moving average is the short-term moving average, the 50-period moving average is the mid-line moving average, and the 100-period moving average is the long-term moving average. The strategy's trading signals come from the intersection of the short-term moving average and the mid-line moving average. The specific logic is that when the long-term moving average is in an upward trend (the long-term moving average price is higher than the mid-line moving average), a buy signal is generated when the short-term moving average crosses the mid-line moving average; a sell signal is generated when the short-term moving average crosses below the mid-line moving average, and the transaction is realized.
## Advantage Analysis
Compared with the conventional double moving average crossover strategy, this strategy adds conditions for mid- and long-term trend judgment before generating trading signals, which can effectively filter out some invalid signals. When the long-term trend is unclear, the strategy will not generate signals and can avoid being trapped. At the same time, this strategy is suitable for capturing trending market conditions in the short and medium term, reducing the possibility of radical entry.
## Risk Analysis
This strategy needs to adjust the period combination of moving averages when setting parameters. Different period combinations will have an impact on the effect of the strategy. If the cycle parameters are not set properly, you run the risk of generating too many false signals. In addition, traders need to be alert to potential systemic risks and stop losses in time to avoid risks.
## Optimization direction
You can consider combining other indicators to assist in judging market trends, such as MACD, BOLL, etc., setting more stringent entry conditions, or combining volatility indicators to build an adaptive moving average so that parameters can be automatically adjusted according to the market environment to further optimize the strategy.
## Summarize
This strategy is based on the conventional double moving average crossover, and adds long-term moving average judgment and filter conditions, which can effectively filter out false signals and is suitable for capturing short- and medium-term trend markets. It is a simple and practical trend following strategy. However, traders still need to pay attention to parameter optimization and systemic risks, and formulate scientific fund management strategies.

## Overview

This strategy optimizes the conventional moving average crossover strategy by setting three moving averages with different periods, constructing a golden cross pattern with 9-period, 50-period and 100-period moving averages. It generates buy signals when the short-term MA crosses above the medium-term MA while the long-term MA is in an uptrend. The strategy name is "Optimized Golden Cross Moving Average Crossover Trading Strategy".  

## Strategy Logic

The strategy utilizes three moving averages with periods of 9, 50 and 100. The 9-period MA is the short-term MA, 50-period MA is the medium-term MA, and 100-period MA is the long-term MA. Trading signals are generated by the crossover between the short-term MA and medium-term MA. Specifically, when the long-term MA is in an uptrend (above the medium-term MA), a buy signal is triggered when the short-term MA crosses above the medium-term MA. A sell signal is triggered when the short-term MA crosses below the medium-term MA. 

## Advantage Analysis

Compared with the conventional dual moving average crossover strategy, this strategy adds the condition of judging medium and long-term trends before generating trading signals, which can effectively filter out some invalid signals. When long-term trends are unclear, the strategy will not generate signals, avoiding being trapped in consolidation. At the same time, this strategy is suitable for capturing trending moves in the short and medium term, reducing the possibility of aggressive entry.

## Risk Analysis

In addition, traders need to be aware of potential systemic risks and timely stop losses to mitigate risks.

//@version=4
strategy("Golden Cross, SMA 100, Moving Average Strategy (by Coinrule)", shorttitle="Golden_Cross_Strat_MA100_optimized", overlay=true, initial_capital = 1000,process_orders_on_close=true, default_qty_type = strategy.percent_of_equity, default_qty_value = 100)

// Input
switch1=input(true, title="Enable Bar Color?")
switch2=input(false, title="Show Fast Moving Average")
switch3=input(true, title="Show Slow Moving Average")

//Calculate Moving Averages
movingaverage_fast = sma(close, input(9))
movingaverage_slow = sma(close, input(100))
movingaverage_normal= sma(close, input(50))

//Backtest dates
fromMonth = input(defval = 1,    title = "From Month",      type = input.integer, minval = 1, maxval = 12)
fromDay   = input(defval = 1,    title = "From Day",        type = input.integer, minval = 1, maxval = 31)
fromYear  = input(defval = 2020, title = "From Year",       type = input.integer, minval = 1970)
thruMonth = input(defval = 1,    title = "Thru Month",      type = input.integer, minval = 1, maxval = 12)
thruDay   = input(defval = 1,    title = "Thru Day",        type = input.integer, minval = 1, maxval = 31)
thruYear  = input(defval = 2112, title = "Thru Year",       type = input.integer, minval = 1970)
showDate  = input(defval = true, title = "Show Date Range", type = input.bool)

start     = timestamp(fromYear, fromMonth, fromDay, 00, 00)        // backtest start window
finish    = timestamp(thruYear, thruMonth, thruDay, 23, 59)        // backtest finish window
window()  => time >= start and time <= finish ? true : false       // create function "within window of time"


// Calculation
bullish_cross = crossover(movingaverage_fast, movingaverage_normal)
bearish_cross = crossunder(movingaverage_fast, movingaverage_normal)

//Entry and Exit
if bullish_cross and window() and movingaverage_slow > movingaverage_normal
    strategy.entry("long", strategy.long)

strategy.close("long", when = bearish_cross and window())

// Colors
bartrendcolor = close > movingaverage_fast and close > movingaverage_slow and change(movingaverage_slow) > 0 ? color.green : close < movingaverage_fast and close < movingaverage_slow and change(movingaverage_slow) < 0 ? color.red : color.blue
barcolor(switch1?bartrendcolor:na)

// Output
plot(movingaverage_fast, color=color.orange, linewidth=2)
plot(movingaverage_slow, color=color.purple, linewidth=3)
plot(movingaverage_normal, color=color.blue, linewidth=2)

bgcolor(color = showDate and window() ? color.gray : na, transp = 90)

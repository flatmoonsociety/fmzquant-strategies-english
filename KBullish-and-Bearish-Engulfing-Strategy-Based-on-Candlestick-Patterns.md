## Strategy Overview

This strategy identifies two forms, bullish engulfing and bearish engulfing, and performs buying or selling operations when the pattern appears, in order to obtain profits brought about by changes in market trends.
## Strategy Principle
The core principle of this strategy is to use the bullish engulfing and bearish engulfing patterns in candle charts to determine changes in market trends. Specifically:
1. Bullish engulfing pattern: The closing price of the current candlestick is higher than the highest price of the previous candlestick, and the opening price of the current candlestick is lower than or equal to the closing price of the previous candlestick. At the same time, the closing price of the current candlestick is lower than or equal to the opening price of the previous candlestick. At this time, a bullish engulfing pattern is formed, indicating that the market may turn from falling to rising.
2. Bearish engulfing pattern: The closing price of the current candlestick is lower than the lowest price of the previous candlestick, and the opening price of the current candlestick is higher than or equal to the closing price of the previous candlestick. At the same time, the closing price of the current candlestick is higher than or equal to the opening price of the previous candlestick. At this time, a bearish engulfing pattern is formed, indicating that the market may turn from rising to falling.
When a bullish engulfing pattern is identified, the strategy will issue a buy signal for long operations; when a bearish engulfing pattern is identified, the strategy will issue a sell signal for short operations. At the same time, the strategy also sets take-profit and stop-loss conditions when holding a position to control risks.

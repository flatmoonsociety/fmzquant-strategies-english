## Overview
This strategy comprehensively uses the price reversal strategy and the relative strength index (RSI) indicator to achieve an organic combination of trend judgment and overbought and oversold judgment. Among them, the price reversal part determines whether the price has a reversal signal, and the RSI part is used to determine whether the market is overbought and oversold. The combination of the two parts of signals can effectively filter out false signals and improve signal quality.
## Strategy Principle
The price reversal part uses the 123 pattern to determine price reversal. Specifically, when the closing price is lower than the closing price of the previous day for 2 consecutive days, and the low channel line of the 9-day stochastic indicator is higher than 50, a buy signal is generated; when the closing price is higher than the closing price of the previous day for 2 consecutive days, and the high channel line of the 9-day stochastic indicator is lower than 50, a sell signal is generated.
The RSI part determines whether the market is overbought or oversold based on whether the relative strength index is above 70 or below 30. RSI above 70 is an overbought signal, and RSI below 30 is an oversold signal.
Finally, the price reversal signal and the RSI signal are logically ANDed. That is, when both are buy signals or sell signals, an actual trading signal is generated to enter the market. This effectively filters out false signals from a single indicator and improves signal quality.
## Strategic Advantages
1. Comprehensive use of multiple indicators to effectively filter false signals
This strategy uses price pattern indicators and overbought and oversold indicators at the same time. The two signals must be in the same direction before entering the market. This can filter out false signals that may be generated by a single indicator to the greatest extent and ensure the reliability of each market entry signal.
2. A trading method that focuses on reversal and auxiliary on trend
The price reversal part uses the 123 pattern to determine the reversal situation. This is a typical reversal trading method. At the same time, the RSI indicator can also determine the trend and play a role in assisting confirmation. The combination of focusing on reversal and supplementing by trend can not only capture reversal opportunities, but also avoid hedging against the trend.
3. Simple parameter setting and easy real-time operation
This strategy only uses two commonly used indicators and has a moderate number of parameters. The overall structure of the strategy is concise and clear, and the actual operation is not difficult and easy to master. This is very important for real traders.

## Risk Analysis
1. Risk of reversal failure
Price reversal trading itself has a probability of failure and cannot be completely avoided. What happens when the price forms a 123 signal, but then reverses back again. This will cause the transaction to fail.
2. Risk of too high trading frequency
The judgment standard of the strategy itself is relatively loose and it is easy to generate more trading signals. If not controlled, it will lead to excessive operation frequency, increase transaction costs and psychological pressure.
3. Improper RSI interval setting
The overbought and oversold range of the RSI indicator defaults to 30-70. These are just empirical parameters. If the actual market situation does not match, it is easy to miss the correct signal or send out the wrong signal.
### Risk resolution
1. Appropriately adjust the position size to control single losses.
2. Add filter conditions and reduce transaction frequency. For example, add moving average judgment.
3. After testing different markets, dynamically adjust the RSI parameter range and set reasonable values.
## Strategy optimization
1. Add moving average indicator judgment
On the existing basis, adding moving average judgment rules can filter out small-scale noise to a certain extent.
2. Optimize RSI parameter settings
By backtesting historical data, test and determine the best parameter combination for RSI overbought and oversold values.
3. Evaluate profit-loss ratio as position exit
In addition to the existing stop-loss methods, an exit mechanism related to the target profit and stop-loss can be added to lock in profits.

## Summarize
This strategy uses double confirmation of price reversal judgment and RSI indicator judgment to achieve a trading idea in which reversal is the main trend and is supplemented by the trend. At the same time, the parameter settings are simple and easy to master the actual offer. // The strategy buys at market, if close price is higher than the previous close 
// during 2 days and the meaning of 9-days Stochastic Slow Oscillator is lower than 50. 
// The strategy sells at market, if close price is lower than the previous close price 
// during 2 days and the meaning of 9-days Stochastic Fast Oscillator is higher than 50.
//
// Second strategy
// The RSI is a very popular indicator that follows price activity. 
// It calculates an average of the positive net changes, and an average 
// of the negative net changes in the most recent bars, and it determines 
// the ratio between these averages. The result is expressed as a number 
// between 0 and 100. Commonly it is said that if the RSI has a low value, 
// for example 30 or under, the symbol is oversold. And if the RSI has a 
// high value, 70 for example, the symbol is overbought. 
//
// WARNING:
// - For purpose educate only
// - This script to change bars colors.
////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////
Reversal123(Length, KSmoothing, DLength, Level) =>
    vFast = sma(stoch(close, high, low, Length), KSmoothing) 
    vSlow = sma(vFast, DLength)
    pos = 0.0
    pos := iff(close[2] < close[1] and close > close[1] and vFast < vSlow and vFast > Level, 1,
	         iff(close[2] > close[1] and close < close[1] and vFast > vSlow and vFast < Level, -1, nz(pos[1], 0))) 
	pos


mRSI(Length,Oversold,Overbought) =>
    pos = 0.0
    xRSI = rsi(close, Length)
    pos:=iff(xRSI > Overbought, 1,
	       iff(xRSI < Oversold, -1, nz(pos[1], 0)))   
    pos

strategy(title="Combo Backtest 123 Reversal & RSI", shorttitle="Combo", overlay = true)

Length = input(14, minval=1)
KSmoothing = input(1, minval=1)
DLength = input(3, minval=1)
Level = input(50, minval=1)
//-------------------------
line2 = input(true, "---- RSI ----")
LengthRSI = input(12, minval=1)
Oversold = input(30, minval=1)
Overbought = input(70, minval=1)
reverse = input(false, title="Trade reverse")
posReversal123 = Reversal123(Length, KSmoothing, DLength, Level)
posmRSI = mRSI(LengthRSI,Oversold,Overbought)
pos = iff(posReversal123 == 1 and posmRSI == 1 , 1,
	   iff(posReversal123 == -1 and posmRSI == -1, -1, 0)) 
possig = iff(reverse and pos == 1, -1,
          iff(reverse and pos == -1 , 1, pos))	   
if (possig == 1 ) 
    strategy.entry("Long", strategy.long)
if (possig == -1 )
    strategy.entry("Short", strategy.short)	 
if (possig == 0) 
    strategy.close_all()
barcolor(possig == -1 ? #b50404: possig == 1 ? #079605 : #0536b3 )

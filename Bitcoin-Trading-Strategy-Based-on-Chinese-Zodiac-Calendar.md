When a bullish signal appears, it is recommended to open a long position; when a bearish signal appears, it is recommended to open a short position; when a wait-and-see signal appears, it is recommended to wait and see temporarily and not blindly open a position.

According to historical data statistics, the Bitcoin market tends to be on an upward trend in years of the Year of the Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, and Snake; and in years of the Year of the Horse, Sheep, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, and Pig, the Bitcoin market tends to be on a downward trend. Therefore, the strategy gives different trading signals based on the remainder.
When the calculated remainder of the year is 0, 1, 4, 5, 8, or 9, it is the zodiac year with an upward trend. The strategy will display a "bullish" signal and it is recommended to open a long position to profit from the rise of the Bitcoin market.
When the calculated remainder of the year is 2, 6, or 10, it is a zodiac year with a downward trend. The strategy will display a "bearish" signal and it is recommended to open a short position to profit from the decline of the Bitcoin market.
When the calculated remainder of the year is 3, 7, or 11, it is a zodiac year with unknown fluctuations. The strategy will display a "wait and see" signal. It is recommended to wait and see the market temporarily and not blindly open positions to avoid unnecessary losses.

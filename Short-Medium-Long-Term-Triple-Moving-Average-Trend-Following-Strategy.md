#### Overview
"Short, medium and long-term triple moving average trend following strategy" is a quantitative investment strategy that uses a combination of moving averages of different periods to capture market trends and conduct transactions. This strategy is based on the 3-day short-term moving average of the lowest price, the 3-day short-term moving average of the highest price, and the 30-day closing price medium-term moving average. It determines the trend direction and sends trading signals by comparing the relative positions of the closing price and these three moving averages. Buy when the closing price falls below the 3-day lowest moving average and is higher than the 30-day closing price moving average, and close the position when the closing price breaks through the 3-day highest moving average.
#### Strategy Principle
The core principle of this strategy is to use the trend characteristics of moving averages and the cross relationship of moving averages of different periods to capture market trends. The short-term 3-day moving average of the lowest price and the highest price can quickly reflect short-term price fluctuations, while the mid-term 30-day moving average of closing prices can reflect the larger-level trend direction.
When the closing price falls below the 3-day moving average of the lowest price and is higher than the 30-day moving average of closing prices, it indicates that there is a short-term retracement but the mid-term trend is still bullish. At this time, enter the market to go long. When the closing price breaks through the 3-day highest price moving average, the short-term upward momentum is exhausted, and the position is closed at this time. Through the combined use of short and medium-term moving averages, the strategy can intervene in the early stages of a trend and exit in time before the end of the trend.

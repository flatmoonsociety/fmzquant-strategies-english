## Overview
This strategy combines Bollinger Bands and moving averages, using the upper and lower rail reversal points of Bollinger Bands and the direction of the moving average as entry and exit signals. Specifically, when the price breaks through the lower track of the Bollinger Band from bottom to top and is above the moving average, go long; when the price breaks through the upper track of the Bollinger Band from top to bottom and is below the moving average, close the position.
## Strategy Principle
This strategy is mainly based on two indicators: Bollinger Bands and Moving Averages.
Bollinger Bands consists of upper band, lower band and middle band. The middle rail is a simple moving average of n days, and the upper and lower bands are k times the standard deviation above and below the middle rail. When the price approaches the upper and lower bands, it indicates overbuying or selling, and a reversal may occur.
Moving averages reflect the average trend direction of prices. When the short-term moving average crosses the long-term moving average, it means that the price trend is from bottom to upward, and you can consider going long; when the short-term moving average crosses below the long-term moving average, it means that the price trend is from top to bottom, and you can consider going short.
This strategy takes into account Bollinger Bands reversal signals and moving average trend judgment. When the price breaks through the lower Bollinger Band, a buy signal is generated, and the moving average is required to rise to ensure that the general trend is upward; when the price breaks through the upper Bollinger Band, a sell signal is generated, and the moving average is required to fall to ensure that the general trend is downward. In this way, the larger trend direction can be considered while reversing.
The specific operating rules are as follows:
1. When the price breaks through the lower Bollinger Band from bottom to top and is above the moving average, go long
2. When the price breaks through the upper Bollinger Band from top to bottom and is lower than the moving average, close the position
## Advantage Analysis
The main advantages of this strategy are:
1. Comprehensive consideration of short- and medium-term reversal signals and long-term trend direction, meeting the needs of long and short bilateral operations
2. Take advantage of the strong reversal of the upper and lower rails of Bollinger Bands to get better entry opportunities.
3. Add moving average filtering to avoid arbitrage in volatile market conditions
4. The strategy logic is simple and clear, easy to understand and implement, and is suitable for quantitative trading.
## Risks and Solutions
The main risks of this strategy are:
1. If the Bollinger Band parameters are set improperly, the trading signals generated by the upper and lower rail breakthroughs may be frequent and it is easy to be trapped. The best parameter combination can be found by optimizing parameters.
2. Improper setting of moving average parameters may filter out better trading opportunities. You can consider combining it with other indicators for optimization. 
3. The market may fluctuate for a long time, leading to increased losses. You can set a stop loss point to control a single loss within a certain range.
## Strategy optimization
The main optimization directions of this strategy are:

> Source (PineScript)

``` pinescript
/*backtest
start: 2023-11-05 00:00:00
end: 2023-12-05 00:00:00
period: 1h
basePeriod: 15m
exchanges: [{"eid":"Futures_Binance","currency":"BTC_USDT"}]
*/

//@version=5
strategy("Bollinger Bands Rejection with MA Trend Filter", overlay=true)

// Bollinger Bands Settings
length = input(20, title="Bollinger Bands Length")
src = input(close, title="Source")
mult = input(2.0, title="Standard Deviation")
basis = ta.sma(src, length)
dev = mult * ta.stdev(src, length)

// Calculate Bollinger Bands
upper_band = basis + dev
lower_band = basis - dev

// MA Settings
ma_length = input(50, title="MA Length")
ma_src = input(close, title="MA Source")
ma = ta.sma(ma_src, ma_length)

// Buy Condition
buy_condition = ta.crossover(close, lower_band) and ta.crossover(close, ma)

// Sell Condition
sell_condition = ta.crossunder(close, upper_band) and ta.crossunder(close, ma)

if buy_condition
    strategy.entry("Buy", strategy.long)
    
if sell_condition
    strategy.close("Buy")

plot(upper_band, color=color.red, title="Upper Bollinger Band")
plot(lower_band, color=color.green, title="Lower Bollinger Band")
plot(ma, color=color.blue, title="50-period MA")

```

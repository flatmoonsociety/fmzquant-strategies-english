## Strategy name
Dual Moving Average Crossover Quantitative Trading Strategy
## Strategy Overview
This strategy makes trading decisions based on the crossover signals of two moving averages (MA) with different periods. When the short-term MA crosses above the long-term MA, a buy signal is generated; when the short-term MA crosses below the long-term MA, a sell signal is generated. This strategy attempts to capture the mid- to long-term trend of prices and gain profits through trend following.
## Strategy Principle
This strategy uses two moving averages with different periods as the main technical indicators. One is a short-term moving average, used to reflect the short-term trend of prices; the other is a long-term moving average, used to reflect the medium and long-term trends of prices. When the short-term MA crosses the long-term MA, it often means that the trend has changed.
Specifically, when the short-term MA crosses the long-term MA, it indicates that the price may enter an upward trend, and the strategy will generate a buy signal at this time. On the contrary, when the short-term MA crosses below the long-term MA, it indicates that the price may enter a downward trend, and the strategy will generate a sell signal at this time. This trend following method can help investors follow market trends and gain profits from rising or falling prices.
In the code implementation of this strategy, the following steps are mainly used:
1. Set the period parameters of short-term MA and long-term MA through the `input` function to facilitate user customization. 
2. Use the `ta.sma` function to calculate the short-term MA.
3. By comparing the closing price with the short-term MA, determine whether the price is above or below the MA.
4. Determine whether a buy or sell signal is generated by judging whether the relationship between the closing price and the short-term MA changes between two consecutive bars.
5. Use the `strategy.entry` ​​function to trade based on buy and sell signals.
6. Use the `plotshape` function to mark buy and sell signals on the chart.
7. Use the `plot` function to draw the short-term MA curve on the chart.
Through the organic combination of these steps, this strategy can dynamically adjust positions according to the crossing changes of the moving average, trying to continue to obtain profits brought by the market trend.
## Strategic Advantages
1. Simple and easy to understand: This strategy uses only one technical indicator, the moving average, and the principle is simple and clear, easy to understand and implement.
2. Strong adaptability: By flexibly setting the period parameters of the two moving averages, it can adapt to different market characteristics and investment needs.
3. Trend following: The strategy is based on moving average crossover to determine the trend, which can effectively capture the mid- to long-term trend of prices and conduct transactions in accordance with the market trend.
4. Easy to optimize: The robustness and profitability of the strategy can be improved by optimizing the period parameters of the moving average.
5. Wide applicability: This strategy can be applied to various financial markets and trading varieties, such as stocks, futures, foreign exchange, etc.
## Strategy Risk
1. Parameter sensitivity: The effect of the strategy is relatively sensitive to the moving average period parameters. Improper parameter settings may lead to performance degradation.
2. Amplitude sensitivity: When the price fluctuation amplitude is large, frequent cross signals may lead to too many transactions and increase costs.
3. Shock market: In a shock market, the price fluctuates frequently above and below the moving average, which may generate more false positive signals.
4. Lagging: The moving average is a lagging indicator. When the cross signal is generated, the price may have been running for a period of time and is slightly lagging behind.
5. Single indicator: This strategy only relies on one indicator, the moving average, which may lack a comprehensive consideration of the market and face certain limitations and risks.
In response to these risks, the following measures can be taken to improve the strategy:
1. Find the best moving average period combination through parameter optimization to improve robustness.
2. Introduce other technical indicators or market signals, such as volume, momentum, etc., to enrich the consideration dimensions of the strategy.
3. Set reasonable stop-profit and stop-loss rules to control the risk of a single transaction.
4. Filter trading signals, such as requiring multiple consecutive K lines to confirm trend changes to reduce false positives.
5. Regularly review and adjust strategies to adapt to dynamic changes in the market.
## Strategy optimization
1. Parameter optimization: You can use methods such as walk forward analysis and grid search to optimize the period parameters of the moving average, find the best parameter combination, and improve the robustness and profitability of the strategy. Optimized cycle parameters can be adjusted according to different market characteristics and investment styles.
2. Signal filtering: After generating a trading signal, some filtering rules can be used to improve the quality of the signal, such as requiring a certain gap between the short-term MA and the long-term MA, requiring the price to have a certain follow through after the MA crosses, requiring simultaneous confirmation of signals in multiple time periods, etc., to reduce false positive signals.
3. Stop-profit and stop-loss: You can set reasonable stop-profit and stop-loss rules for each transaction. On the one hand, you can prevent the downside risk of a single transaction, and on the other hand, you can lock in profits in a timely manner. The positions of stop-profit and stop-loss can be dynamically adjusted based on factors such as price votingability, support, and resistance.
4. Position management: The position size of each transaction can be dynamically adjusted based on the strength of the market trend, account risk tolerance and other factors, increasing the position when the trend is strong and reducing the position when the trend weakens to better adapt to the market.
5. Combination of multiple indicators: You can use other technical indicators or market signals in conjunction with moving averages, such as MACD, RSI, ATR, etc., to judge and confirm trends from multiple dimensions to improve the reliability of the strategy. The weights between different indicators can be adjusted according to their stability under different market conditions.
The purpose of these optimization directions is to improve the adaptability, robustness and profitability of the strategy and better respond to market changes and challenges. Through continuous optimization and improvement, the strategy can achieve better results in practical applications.

``` pinescript
//@version=5
strategy("SMA Crossover Strategy", overlay=true)

// SMA parametrelerini ayarla
sma_short_length = input.int(15, "Kısa SMA Uzunluğu")
sma_long_length = input.int(200, "Uzun SMA Uzunluğu")

// Hareketli ortalama hesaplamalarını yap
sma_short = ta.sma(close, sma_short_length)

// Fiyatın SMA'yı yukarı veya aşağı kestiğini kontrol et
price_above_sma = close > sma_short
price_below_sma = close < sma_short

// Alım-Satım noktalarını belirle
longCondition = (close[1] < sma_short[1] and close > sma_short) and price_above_sma
shortCondition = (close[1] > sma_short[1] and close < sma_short) and price_below_sma

// Al-Sat stratejisi
if (longCondition)
    strategy.entry("Long", strategy.long)
if (shortCondition)
    strategy.entry("Short", strategy.short)

// Fiyatın kısa SMA'yı yukarı kesme noktalarını göster
plotshape(series=longCondition, title="Long", location=location.belowbar, color=color.green, style=shape.triangleup, size=size.small)

// Fiyatın kısa SMA'yı aşağı kesme noktalarını göster
plotshape(series=shortCondition, title="Short", location=location.abovebar, color=color.red, style=shape.triangledown, size=size.small)

// Hareketli ortalamaları grafiğe çiz
plot(sma_short, color=color.blue, title="Kısa SMA")
```

## Overview
The momentum breakout moving average strategy is a stock trading strategy that uses moving average crossover signals combined with the momentum indicator. This strategy comprehensively uses multiple technical indicators such as the exponential moving average (EMA), simple moving average (SMA), moving average convergence index (MACD), and modified relative strength index (StockRSI) to form a buy signal on the premise of confirming that the long-term trend is upward. When short-term momentum indicators show a reversal signal, the strategy takes a stop loss.
## Strategy Principle
The strategy mainly consists of the following components:
1. **EMA/SMA moving average crossover**: Set the 9-period EMA fast line and the 21-period SMA slow line. When the fast line crosses the slow line, a buy signal is generated.
2. **MACD indicator**: The MACD indicator includes MACD line, signal line and MACD column. Acts as additional confirmation when the bar is positive and synchronized with the buy signal generated by the EMA/SMA.
3. **StockRSI indicator**: StockRSI is an improved version of the RSI indicator. A buy signal is generated when the indicator line is above the OVERBOUGHT line (80) or below the OVERSOLD line (20).
4. **Bollinger Bands**: The middle track of Bollinger Bands is the 20-day SMA, and the bandwidth is two standard deviations above and below. A trading signal is generated when the price is within the Bollinger Bands.
5. **Stop Loss and Take Profit Levels**: Calculate the stop loss and take profit prices based on the ATR of the past 14 days.
This strategy requires that at least two of the above indicators must send out buy signals at the same time, and the stock price is within the Bollinger Bands and the long-term trend is upward, before the final buy signal is generated. When the MACD indicator reverses downwards and StockRSI enters the overbought area, a sell signal is generated.
## Advantage Analysis
This strategy combines the advantages of moving average crossovers, momentum indicators, and volatility indicators and has the following main advantages:
1. **Excellent backtest results**: Multiple indicators are all mature indicators, and the strategy results are better than the market and single indicators.
2. **Parameters optimized**: Main parameters such as EMA period and Bollinger Bands channel have been optimized to improve system stability.
3. **Automatic stop loss/take profit**: Bollinger Bands and ATR can adjust the stop loss level in real time, which is beneficial to risk control.
4. **Not difficult to implement**: The code is relatively concise, the indicators are easy to obtain, and the actual operation is not difficult.
## Risk Analysis
Although this strategy has performed well, it still carries the following major risks:
1. **The indicator sends an error signal**: When the market experiences abnormal fluctuations or the indicator fails, an error signal may occur. At this time, the long-term trend should be judged.
2. **Improper parameters**: Improper parameter settings may lead to too frequent transactions or insensitive responses. It should be adjusted according to different varieties and market environment.
3. **Stop loss is too small or too large**: If the stop loss is too small, it is easy to get trapped, but if the stop loss is too large, the loss will be too large. Stop loss and take profit should be balanced.
In response to the above risks, the following measures can be taken to deal with them:
1. **Manual intervention and modification**: In the event of abnormal conditions, the signal can be manually confirmed, parameters can be modified or the strategy can be suspended.
2. **Optimization parameter settings**: Use more scientific and objective methods such as genetic algorithms to optimize parameters.
3. **Adjust stop loss based on volatility**: The stop loss range can be automatically adjusted based on volatility, such as 1-3 times ATR.
## Optimization direction
This strategy can be further optimized from the following aspects:
1. **Increase the stability of the stop loss mechanism**: Trailing stop loss or exponential moving average can be added to stop loss.
2. **Combined with trading volume filtering**: Add trading volume indicators to avoid invalid breakthroughs.
3. **Dynamic adjustment parameters**: The moving average period, channel width, etc. can be automatically optimized according to the market environment.
4. **Introduction of machine learning algorithms**: Use RNN, LSTM and other algorithms to achieve dynamic optimization of parameters.
## Summarize
The momentum breakthrough moving average strategy comprehensively uses the advantages of technical indicators and achieved good returns with the combination of long and short runs. This strategy has good stop loss control and is not difficult to implement. The next step will be to further improve the stop-loss mechanism and use more intelligent methods for parameter optimization and signal filtering, striving to achieve more stable excess returns.

## Overview

The Momentum Breakout Moving Average strategy is a stock trading strategy that combines moving average crossover signals with momentum indicators. The strategy employs multiple technical indicators including Exponential Moving Average (EMA), Simple Moving Average (SMA), Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD), and a modified Relative Strength Index (StockRSI) to generate buy signals when confirming an upward long-term trend. When short-term momentum indicators show reversal signals, the strategy takes profit.

## Strategy Logic  

The key components of this strategy are:   

1. **EMA/SMA Crossover**: A 9-period EMA fast line crosses above a 21-period SMA slow line to trigger a buy signal.  

2. **MACD Indicator**: MACD histogram needs to be positive when combined with the EMA/SMA crossover signal as additional confirmation.

3. **StockRSI Indicator**: Signals are triggered when StockRSI is above the OVERBOUGHT level (80) or below the OVERSOLD level (20).   

4. **Bollinger Bands**: Require the price to be within the bands where the middle band is a 20-period SMA and the width of bands is two standard deviations.  

5. **Stop Loss and Take Profit**: Calculated based on 14-period ATR.   

The strategy requires at least 2 out of the 3 indicators to give buy signals, price is within the Bollinger Bands, and the long-term trend remains bullish to generate the final buy signal. It gives a sell signal when MACD histogram turns negative and StockRSI enters the overbought region.  

## Advantage Analysis   

The key strengths of this strategy are:  

1. **Excellent Backtest Results**: Multiple proven indicators lead to outperformance over benchmark and individual indicators. 

2. **Optimized Parameters**: Key parameters like EMA periods and Bollinger Bands are optimized for improved stability.

1. **Manual Intervention**: In abnormal situations, signals can be manually checked, parameters readjusted or strategies temporarily halted.  

2. **Parameter Optimization**: More scientific and objective methods like genetic algorithms can be employed for systematic optimization.

//@version=5
strategy("Improved Custom Strategy", shorttitle="ICS", overlay=true)

// Volatility
volatility = ta.atr(14)

// EMA/MA Crossover
fast_length = 9
slow_length = 21
fast_ma = ta.ema(close, fast_length)
slow_ma = ta.sma(close, slow_length)
crossover_signal = ta.crossover(fast_ma, slow_ma)

// MACD
[macdLine, signalLine, macdHistogram] = ta.macd(close, 12, 26, 9)
macd_signal = crossover_signal and (macdHistogram > 0)

// Bollinger Bands
source = close
basis = ta.sma(source, 20)
upper = basis + 2 * ta.stdev(source, 20)
lower = basis - 2 * ta.stdev(source, 20)

// Fractal-based Support and Resistance levels
isFractalHigh = high[2] < high[1] and high[1] > high[0]
isFractalLow = low[2] > low[1] and low[1] < low[0]
resistance = ta.valuewhen(isFractalHigh, high[1], 0)
support = ta.valuewhen(isFractalLow, low[1], 0)

// StockRSI
length = 14
K = 100 * (close - ta.lowest(close, length)) / (ta.highest(close, length) - ta.lowest(close, length))
D = ta.sma(K, 3)
overbought = 80
oversold = 20
stockrsi_signal = ((K < D) and (K < oversold)) or ((K > D) and (K > overbought))

// Buy and sell conditions
mandatory_buy_conditions = (crossover_signal ? 1 : 0) + (macd_signal ? 1 : 0) + (stockrsi_signal ? 1 : 0)

// Long-term Trend Check
long_term_ma = ta.sma(close, 200)
long_term_bullish = close > long_term_ma
long_term_bearish = close < long_term_ma

// Plot the long-term MA for visual clarity
plot(long_term_ma, color=color.gray, title="200-Day MA", linewidth=1)

// Simplified Buy and Sell conditions
buy_condition = long_term_bullish and (mandatory_buy_conditions >= 2) and (close > lower) and (close < upper)
sell_condition = (macdHistogram < 0) and (K > D) and (K > overbought)


// Potential SL and TP based on volatility
potential_SL = close - volatility
potential_TP = close + 2 * volatility

plot(potential_SL, title="SL Level", color=color.red, linewidth=1, style=plot.style_linebr)
plot(potential_TP, title="TP Level", color=color.green, linewidth=1, style=plot.style_linebr)

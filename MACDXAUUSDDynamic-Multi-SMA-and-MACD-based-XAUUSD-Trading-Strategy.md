## Strategy Overview
This strategy is a XAUUSD trading strategy that combines the moving average (SMA) and the moving average convergence indicator (MACD). The strategy uses SMAs of different periods to determine trend direction and potential entry points, and uses the MACD indicator to confirm that the direction of momentum is consistent with the signal generated by the SMA crossover. In addition, the strategy also uses average true range (ATR) to set dynamic stop loss and take profit levels to adapt to different market fluctuations.
## Strategy Principle
The core principles of this strategy can be divided into three parts:
1. **Trend Judgment**: The strategy uses the 100-period SMA to determine the overall trend direction. When the price is above the SMA, it is considered to be in an upward trend and consider going long; when the price is below the SMA, it is considered to be in a downtrend and consider shorting. In addition, the strategy also uses a 15-period fast SMA and a 45-period slow SMA, using their crossover to identify more immediate trend changes and potential entry points.
2. **Momentum Confirmation**: The strategy uses the MACD(12, 26, 9) indicator to confirm that the momentum direction is consistent with the entry signal generated by the SMA crossover. When there is a positive divergence between the MACD line and the signal line (the MACD line crosses the signal line), it is supported to go long; when there is a negative divergence between the MACD line and the signal line (the MACD line crosses below the signal line), it is supported to go short.
3. **Risk Management**: The strategy uses ATR (14 periods) to set dynamic stop loss and take profit levels to adapt to current market fluctuations. The stop loss position is set at a position 3 times ATR from the entry price, while the take profit target is set at a position 6 times ATR from the entry price (twice the stop loss distance), in order to obtain a risk-reward ratio of 2:1.
The bull entry conditions for this strategy are: a close above the 100-period trending SMA, the 15-period fast SMA crossing above the 45-period slow SMA, and the MACD line above the signal line (indicating bullish momentum). Short entry conditions are: a close below the 100-period trending SMA, the 15-period fast SMA below the 45-period slow SMA, and the MACD line below the signal line (indicating bearish momentum).

strategy("Refined XAUUSD SMA and MACD Strategy", overlay=true, default_qty_type=strategy.percent_of_equity, default_qty_value=10)

// Moving Averages for trend direction and entry signals
trendSMA = ta.sma(close, 100) // Trend direction SMA
fastSMA = ta.sma(close, 15)
slowSMA = ta.sma(close, 45)

// MACD parameters for entry signal strength
[macdLine, signalLine, _] = ta.macd(close, 12, 26, 9)

// ATR for dynamic stop loss and take profit
atrPeriod = 14
atrMultiplier = 3.0
atr = ta.atr(atrPeriod)

// Entry conditions with trend filter and stronger MACD divergence
longCondition = close > trendSMA and ta.crossover(fastSMA, slowSMA) and (macdLine - signalLine) > 0
shortCondition = close < trendSMA and ta.crossunder(fastSMA, slowSMA) and (signalLine - macdLine) > 0

// Dynamic stop loss and take profit based on ATR
dynamicSL = atr * atrMultiplier
dynamicTP = atr * atrMultiplier * 2 // Aiming for a 2:1 risk-reward ratio

if (longCondition)
    strategy.entry("Long", strategy.long)
    strategy.exit("Exit Long", "Long", stop=close - dynamicSL, limit=close + dynamicTP)

if (shortCondition)
    strategy.entry("Short", strategy.short)
    strategy.exit("Exit Short", "Short", stop=close + dynamicSL, limit=close - dynamicTP)

// Plotting
plot(trendSMA, color=color.purple)
plot(fastSMA, color=color.red)
plot(slowSMA, color=color.blue)
hline(0, "Zero Line", color=color.gray)
plot(macdLine - signalLine, color=color.green, title="MACD Histogram")
plot(macdLine, color=color.blue, title="MACD Line")
plot(signalLine, color=color.orange, title="Signal Line")

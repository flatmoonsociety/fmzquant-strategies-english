## Overview
The EMA and SuperTrend combined trend following strategy cleverly combines the EMA indicator and the SuperTrend indicator to identify market trends and give clear entry and exit signals. This strategy customizes SuperTrend parameters and EMA parameters to suit different trading styles. Color-coded K lines make identifying trends intuitive. The strategy determines entry timing based on the cross signal between the EMA and SuperTrend indicators. The exit rules are flexible and can be exited when the trend reverses or when the price crosses the EMA again, which is helpful for risk management.
## Strategy Principle
This strategy uses the SuperTrend indicator to determine the main trend direction. The SuperTrend indicator essentially combines the ATR indicator with the moving average, and uses the price to break through the average to determine the trend turning point. On the other hand, the role of the EMA indicator is to assist in determining the short-term trend direction. A long position will only be considered when the price is above the EMA, and a short position will be considered only when the price is below the EMA.
Specifically, the strategy adopts the following judgment rules:
1. Long signal: when the price breaks through the SuperTrend line upward and is above the EMA;
2. Short signal: when the price breaks through the SuperTrend line downward and is below the EMA;
3. Close long positions: when the price breaks through the SuperTrend line or is below the EMA;
4. Close short positions: when the price breaks through the SuperTrend line upward or is above the EMA.
## Advantage Analysis
### Stable trend identification
The combination of EMA and SuperTrend makes trend judgment more reliable. Compared with a single indicator, this combination can more effectively filter out some false breakthroughs.
### Flexible parameter adjustment
Allows you to customize the parameters of SuperTrend and EMA to adapt to different varieties and different time periods.
### Clear entry and exit signals
The long and short signals given by the strategy are relatively clear and helpful for decision-making. The exit rules are also relatively clear, which facilitates risk control.
### Intuitive interface expression
According to the position relationship between price and EMA, the K line appears in different colors, forming an intuitive visual effect.
## Risk Analysis
### May miss some opportunities
Compared with a single indicator, this strategy has higher requirements for signal confirmation, so it may miss opportunities where the callback is not deep.
### The risk of false breakthrough still exists
Although this strategy has verified the breakthrough signal, when the market fluctuates violently, there is still the possibility of false breakthroughs, causing the strategy to fail.
### The effect is related to parameter settings
The parameter settings of SuperTrend and EMA will have a great impact on the strategy effect. If the parameters are set improperly, there will be multiple exits and openings.
## Optimization direction
1. Filter signals in combination with other indicators
You can consider adding other indicators such as MACD, Stochastics, etc. to form a combination with EMA and SuperTrend to further verify the signal and reduce the probability of false breakthroughs.
2. Multi-time period verification
Determine the trend direction in a higher time period (such as the daily line) to verify and screen the signals generated by the current period.
3. Automatic parameter optimization
You can try to traverse different parameter combinations and automatically select the optimal parameter settings for a given variety and time period based on backtest indicators.
## Summarize
The EMA and SuperTrend combined trend following strategy successfully combines the advantages of trend judgment and trend following. The strategy's stable, clear signals and customizable parameters provide strong support for trading decisions. Of course, we still need to be wary of possible false breakthrough risks. The strategy effect can be further improved by adding other indicators or multi-time period analysis for verification.

> Source (PineScript)

``` pinescript
/*backtest
start: 2022-12-04 00:00:00
end: 2023-12-10 00:00:00
period: 1d
basePeriod: 1h
exchanges: [{"eid":"Futures_Binance","currency":"BTC_USDT"}]
*/

//@version=5
strategy("EMA SuperTrend Strategy", overlay=true)

// SuperTrend EMA Settings
length = input.int(10, title="SuperTrend Length")
mult = input.float(2.0, title="Multiplier")
emaLength = input.int(34, title="EMA UpTrend", minval=1, maxval=300)

// EMA calculation for EMA Trend Bars
ema1 = input.int(34, title="EMA UpTrend", minval=1, maxval=300)
shema = input(true, title="Show EMA Trend is Based On?")

usedEma = ta.ema(close, ema1)

// EMA Trend Bars color
emaUpColor() => hlc3 >= usedEma
emaDownColor() => hlc3 < usedEma
col = hlc3 >= usedEma ? color.lime : hlc3 < usedEma ? color.red : color.white

// SuperTrend calculation
atrPeriod = int(mult)
[supertrend, direction] = ta.supertrend(length, atrPeriod)

// Entry conditions
longEntry = ta.crossover(close, supertrend) and close > usedEma
shortEntry = ta.crossunder(close, supertrend) and close < usedEma

// Exit conditions
longExit = ta.crossunder(close, supertrend) or close < usedEma
shortExit = ta.crossover(close, supertrend) or close > usedEma

// Execute trades
if (longEntry)
    strategy.entry("Buy", strategy.long)

if (longExit)
    strategy.close("Buy")

if (shortEntry)
    strategy.entry("Sell", strategy.short)

if (shortExit)
    strategy.close("Sell")

// Plotting
plot(shema and usedEma ? usedEma : na, title="EMA", style=plot.style_line, linewidth=3, color=col)

```

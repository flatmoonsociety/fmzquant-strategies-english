#### Overview
The "short-term short-term highly liquid currency pair strategy" aims to take advantage of the short-term downward trend of highly liquid currency pairs and conduct short-selling transactions when prices are expected to fall in order to obtain profits. This strategy enters short positions based on specific conditions and uses dynamic position sizing and risk management measures to control risk and lock in profits.
The main ideas of this strategy are as follows:
1. Select a currency pair with high liquidity as the trading target.
2. Enter a short position based on the price drop percentage condition.
3. Dynamically calculate the position size and control risks based on a certain proportion of account equity.
4. Set stop loss and take profit conditions to limit potential losses and lock in profits.
5. Exit a trade based on trade duration or price change conditions.
#### Strategy Principle
This strategy takes advantage of short-term downtrends in highly liquid currency pairs. The strategy enters a short position when the price meets certain conditions. The specific principles are as follows:
1. Make sure there are no open transactions currently to ensure there is only one active transaction at a time.
2. Set the duration of short trade, the default is 7 days.
3. Enter a short position when the price falls by a predetermined percentage (default is 30%) from the entry price.
4. Dynamically calculate the position size based on the predetermined risk percentage of account equity, and control the fund allocation and overall risk of each transaction.
5. Set stop loss and take profit conditions. When the price moves in an unfavorable direction, the strategy will exit the transaction to minimize losses; when the price moves in a favorable direction, the strategy will exit the transaction to lock in profits.
6. Exit a trade based on trade duration or price movement conditions.

1. Short-term trading: This strategy focuses on capturing the short-term downward trend of highly circulated currency pairs. The trading cycle is relatively short, which helps to quickly achieve profit targets.
2. Dynamic position size: dynamically calculate the position size based on the predetermined risk percentage of account equity, effectively control the risk exposure of each transaction, and adapt to different market conditions.
3. Risk management: Set stop loss and take profit conditions, exit the transaction promptly when the price moves in an unfavorable direction, and minimize potential losses; lock in profits when the price moves in a favorable direction, and protect the realized gains.
4. Simple and easy to use: The conditions and logic of this strategy are relatively simple, easy to understand and implement, and are suitable for traders with different experience levels.

3. Parameter optimization risk: The performance of this strategy depends on the selection of multiple parameters, such as short duration, price drop percentage, stop loss and take profit percentage, etc. Improper parameter settings can lead to poor strategy performance.

//@version=5
strategy("Short High-Grossing Forex Pair", overlay=true)

// Parameters
shortDuration = input.int(7, title="Short Duration (days)")
priceDropPercentage = input.float(30, title="Price Drop Percentage", minval=0, maxval=100)
riskPerTrade = input.float(1, title="Risk per Trade (%)", minval=0.1, maxval=100) / 100  // Risk per trade as a percentage of equity
stopLossPercent = input.float(5, title="Stop Loss Percentage", minval=0)  // Stop Loss Percentage
takeProfitPercent = input.float(30, title="Take Profit Percentage", minval=0)  // Take Profit Percentage

// Initialize variables
var int shortEnd = na
var float entryPrice = na

// Calculate dynamic position size
equity = strategy.equity
riskAmount = equity * riskPerTrade
pipValue = syminfo.pointvalue
stopLossPips = close * (stopLossPercent / 100)
positionSize = riskAmount / (stopLossPips * pipValue)

// Entry condition: Enter short position at the first bar with calculated position size
if (strategy.opentrades == 0)
    strategy.entry("Short", strategy.short, qty=positionSize)
    shortEnd := bar_index + shortDuration
    entryPrice := close
    alert("Entering short position", alert.freq_once_per_bar_close)

// Exit conditions
exitCondition = (bar_index >= shortEnd) or (close <= entryPrice * (1 - priceDropPercentage / 100))

// Stop-loss and take-profit conditions
stopLossCondition = (close >= entryPrice * (1 + stopLossPercent / 100))
takeProfitCondition = (close <= entryPrice * (1 - takeProfitPercent / 100))

// Exit the short position based on the conditions
if (strategy.opentrades > 0 and (exitCondition or stopLossCondition or takeProfitCondition))
    strategy.close("Short")
    alert("Exiting short position", alert.freq_once_per_bar_close)

// Plot entry and exit points for visualization
plotshape(series=strategy.opentrades > 0, location=location.belowbar, color=color.red, style=shape.labeldown, text="Short")
plotshape(series=strategy.opentrades == 0, location=location.abovebar, color=color.green, style=shape.labelup, text="Exit")

// Add alert conditions
alertcondition(strategy.opentrades > 0, title="Short Entry Alert", message="Entering short position")
alertcondition(strategy.opentrades == 0, title="Short Exit Alert", message="Exiting short position")

## Overview
Dual Reversal Overlap Selective Strategy achieves asset allocation and timing trading by combining reversal trading strategies and overbought and oversold screening. The strategy is designed to buy and sell at trend reversal points while using overbought and oversold indicators to avoid unnecessary trades in irrational expansion zones.
## Strategy Principle
This strategy consists of two sub-strategies superimposed:
1. 123 reversal strategy
This strategy is based on two consecutive closing price reversal trading signals. Specifically, if the closing price rises in the last two days and the 9-day slow K-line stoch value is lower than 50, go long; if the closing price falls in the last two days and the 9-day fast K-line stoch value is higher than 50, go short. This strategy is a reversal strategy designed to capture short-term trend reversals.
2. Bressart Double Smoothed Oscillator Strategy (DSS)
This strategy uses the Bressatt Double Smoothed Oscillator to determine overbought and oversold conditions. Specifically, if the 5-day moving average is below the 10-day moving average and below the oversold zone of 20, go long; if the 5-day moving average is above the 10-day moving average and above the oversold zone of 80, go short. This strategy is an overbought and oversold strategy and aims to avoid unnecessary trading in irrational areas.
The final signal is generated by the combination of the two, and the transaction will be triggered only when the two give consistent signals. This can increase the probability of profit and take advantage of the advantages of two different types of strategies to combine.
## Strategic advantage analysis
1. Combining the advantages of reversal strategy and overbought and oversold strategy, it can not only capture short-term trend reversal, but also avoid irrational area trading.
2. The 123 reversal strategy has fewer parameters, simple logic, and is easy to implement. The DSS strategy uses double exponential smoothing to determine overbought and oversold, which can effectively filter out short signals in the long market and long signals in the short market.
3. The combination of two different types of strategies can improve the reliability of signals and reduce false signals of the original strategy.
4. Flexible strategy parameter setting, the parameters can be adjusted according to different markets, and the adaptability is strong.
## Strategy risk analysis
1. The reversal strategy itself has the risk of "picking up coins" and can easily get stuck in a volatile market.
2. The DSS strategy has the problem of difficulty in parameter optimization, and different parameters have a greater impact on the results.
3. When the signals of the two strategies are inconsistent, there is a risk of missing trading opportunities.
4. The strategy is only based on simple price indicators, lacks comprehensive judgment, and has certain profit limitations.
Corresponding solutions:
1. Appropriately shorten the position period and reduce the risk of hold-up.
2. Learn from successful cases and carefully test parameter combinations to optimize parameters for specific markets.
3. Consider adding other auxiliary judgment indicators to improve the effectiveness of the strategy.
4. Optimize entry timing, or adjust position ratio.
## Strategy optimization direction

2. Try other overbought and oversold indicators to replace DSS, such as energy tide, RSI, etc.
3. Add a stop-loss strategy to lock in profits and reduce losses.

## Summarize
The two-way reversal overlapping optimization strategy realizes the dual functions of asset allocation and timing trading through the combination of reversal strategy and overbought and oversold strategy. The strategy has the advantages of flexible parameters, simple logic, and easy implementation, and can effectively filter out noise transactions in irrational areas. 1. 123 Reversal Strategy  

This strategy uses reversal signals based on two consecutive days of closing price reversal. Specifically, it goes long if closing prices rise over the last two days and the 9-day slow stochastic is below 50, and goes short if closing prices fall over the last two days and the 9-day fast stochastic is above 50. This reversal strategy aims to capture short-term trend reversals.

This overbought-oversold strategy helps avoid unnecessary trades in irrational zones. 

The final signal is generated only when both strategies agree. This improves profitability by combining the strengths of the two strategy types.

Reversal123(Length, KSmoothing, DLength, Level) =>
    vFast = sma(stoch(close, high, low, Length), KSmoothing) 
    vSlow = sma(vFast, DLength)
    pos = 0.0
    pos := iff(close[2] < close[1] and close > close[1] and vFast < vSlow and vFast > Level, 1,
	         iff(close[2] > close[1] and close < close[1] and vFast > vSlow and vFast < Level, -1, nz(pos[1], 0))) 
	pos

DSSB(PDS, EMAlen,TriggerLen,Overbought,Oversold) =>
    pos = 0
    xPreCalc = ema(stoch(close, high, low, PDS), EMAlen)
    xDSS = ema(stoch(xPreCalc, xPreCalc, xPreCalc, PDS), EMAlen)
    xTrigger = ema(xDSS, TriggerLen)
    pos := iff(xTrigger < xDSS and xTrigger < Oversold, -1,
	         iff(xTrigger > xDSS and xTrigger > Overbought, 1, nz(pos[1], 0))) 
    pos

strategy(title="Combo Backtest 123 Reversal & DSS Bressert", shorttitle="Combo", overlay = true)

DLength = input(3, minval=1)
Level = input(50, minval=1)
//-------------------------
PDS = input(10, minval=1)
EMAlen = input(9, minval=1)
TriggerLen = input(5, minval=1)
Overbought = input(80, minval=1)
Oversold = input(20, minval=1)
reverse = input(false, title="Trade reverse")
posReversal123 = Reversal123(Length, KSmoothing, DLength, Level)

	   iff(posReversal123 == -1 and posDSS == -1, -1, 0)) 
possig = iff(reverse and pos == 1, -1,
          iff(reverse and pos == -1 , 1, pos))	   
if (possig == 1) 
    strategy.entry("Long", strategy.long)
if (possig == -1)
    strategy.entry("Short", strategy.short)	 
if (possig == 0) 
    strategy.close_all()
